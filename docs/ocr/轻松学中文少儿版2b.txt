Dragon Boat Festival
Dragon Boat Festival is celebrated on the fifth day of the fifth
lunar month. Chinese New Year and Mid-Autumn Festival,
together with Dragon Boat Festival, form the three major
Chinese traditional festivals. Dragon Boat Festival is highlighted
by dragon boat races, in which competing teams row their boats
forward to the rhythm of pounding drums. The most popular
food eaten on Dragon Boat Festival is "zongzi (*7 3)". It was
originally made and eaten in memory of the patriotic poet Qu
Yuan (ff). However, it has gradually evolved into a snack
eaten during normal occasions as well.
