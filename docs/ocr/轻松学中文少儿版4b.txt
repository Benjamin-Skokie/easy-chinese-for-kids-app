Chinese
Painting
Chinese painting is one of the oldest continuous artistic traditions in the
world. The tools used in Chinese painting are: paintbrush, ink, Xuan paper
(rice paper) and inkstone. Chinese _paintings are generally categorized
into three themes: figures (11, rénwùhud), landscapes (1, * I
shānshuthud), and birds-and-flowers (1E 1, I, huaniăohu). Popular
examples of figures include images of immortals, emperors and common
people. The common subjects in the birds-and-flowers type of painting are
flowers, bamboos, birds, insects and stones.
