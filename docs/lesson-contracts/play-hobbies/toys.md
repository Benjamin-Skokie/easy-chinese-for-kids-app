## Confirmed language and scope

The 2A source teaches `我有 + 玩具` (“I have + toy”), not `这是我的…`. Its four toy nouns are `娃娃`, `玩具火车`, `玩具飞机`, and `玩具熊`. It models `我有娃娃。`, `我有玩具火车。`, `我有玩具飞机。`, and `我有玩具熊。`, then extends ownership to people: `乐乐有娃娃。`, `乐乐的弟弟有玩具熊。`, and `乐乐的弟弟有玩具火车。`

The word `玩具` is taught explicitly as the reusable category word “toy.” It makes compounds such as `玩具汽车`, `玩具火车`, `玩具飞机`, and `玩具校车` transparent. The phrase `这是我的…` remains only a mapping inference and is rejected for this page. `玩具汽车`, `玩具校车`, and `自行车` are enrichment rather than book words; they are available in the optional picture-card and picture-choice extension, while the confirmed 2A core remains the four book nouns.

## Asset decision

The existing isolated WebPs (`toy-doll.webp`, `train.webp`, `airplane.webp`,
and `teddy-bear.webp`) cover the four visible toy nouns, but their native
portrait and landscape ratios are mixed and none teaches ownership. Reuse them
only for fixed-frame practice cues. Generate a consistent 4:3 Lele scene
series for the Scene grid; do not make a grid from the mismatched source
assets. Retire the single all-toys overview scene once the four-scene series
is ready.

Use the mixed-ratio isolated images only in fixed 4:3 flashcard frames with
`object-fit: contain`, matching the Food page's picture-card treatment.

The reviewed scene series is `lele-playing-doll.webp`,
`lele-playing-toy-train.webp`, `lele-playing-toy-airplane.webp`, and
`lele-playing-toy-bear.webp`. Each is 960 × 720 (4:3) and uses the same Lele,
outfit, home-playroom setting, and visual scale.
