## Confirmed language and scope

The 2A source teaches `我有 + 玩具` (“I have + toy”), not `这是我的…`. Its four toy nouns are `娃娃`, `玩具火车`, `玩具飞机`, and `玩具熊`. It models `我有娃娃。`, `我有玩具火车。`, `我有玩具飞机。`, and `我有玩具熊。`, then extends ownership to people: `乐乐有娃娃。`, `乐乐的弟弟有玩具熊。`, and `乐乐的弟弟有玩具火车。`

The phrase `这是我的…` remains only a mapping inference and is rejected for this page. `玩具汽车`, `校车`, and `自行车` are also out of scope: the book's toy lesson does not teach them, and the latter two belong to later transport language.
