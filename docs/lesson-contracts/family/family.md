## Current contract

The page owns parent and sibling vocabulary, family scenes, and early ownership/counting frames. Build its `我有… / 我没有…` practice from the family-specific two-younger-brothers scene; do not reuse the unrelated schoolbag `有 / 没有` grammar visual.

## Lesson evidence

+- **1B Lesson 1 — Mum and dad:** Words: `爸爸、妈妈、爱`. Sentences: `我爱爸爸。` / `我爱妈妈。`.
- **1B Lesson 2 — Brothers and sisters:** Words: `哥哥、姐姐、弟弟、妹妹`. Task: identify family members.
- **2A Lesson 2 — Two younger brothers:** Words: `一个、两个、没有`. Frames: `我有…` / `我没有…。`.
- **3A Lesson 4 — Family:** `我家有五口人。爸爸、妈妈、哥哥、弟弟和我。` Words: `爷爷、奶奶、外公、外婆`. Task: introduce family members from a family photo.
