## Cross-book progression

| Book lesson | Language job |
| --- | --- |
| 1B Lesson 1 — Mum and dad | Name parents and say family affection. |
| 1B Lesson 2 — Brothers and sisters | Name siblings; use `我有…` / `我没有…` with `一个` and `两个`. |
| 2A Lesson 2 — Two younger brothers | Revisit sibling counts and possession. |
| 3A Lesson 4 — Family | Broaden family description. |

## Page blueprint

| Section | Final decision |
| --- | --- |
| Scene | Existing 1B sibling scenes, then the 2A dinner scene with three child lines: `我有两个弟弟。` / `我有一个姐姐和一个弟弟。` / `我有一个姐姐和一个哥哥。`; the order follows the left, middle, and right children in the picture without displaying speaker-position labels. End with a natural six-person family portrait: `这是我的家人。` / `我家有六口人。` The 3A five-person model stays in spoken practice rather than describing this broader family image. |
| Book words | Parent, sibling, grandparent, and 2A count/ownership words: `有、没有、一个、两个`; text-and-portrait cards only. |
| Useful note | Explain `有` with `一个` / `两个` for describing siblings, plus `口` for counting people in a family. |
| Practice | Keep the family-word matching review, then let the learner choose household size separately and `没有 / 一个 / 两个` for each sibling type. Build a natural `我有…` / `我没有…` description, and read the complete learner-built sentence after each choice. Household size is not calculated from assumed parents, so the activity supports single-parent and other family structures. |

## Current contract

The page owns parent and sibling vocabulary, family scenes, and early ownership/counting frames. Build its `我有… / 我没有…` practice from the family-specific two-younger-brothers scene; do not reuse the unrelated schoolbag `有 / 没有` grammar visual.

## Lesson evidence

+- **1B Lesson 1 — Mum and dad:** Words: `爸爸、妈妈、爱`. Sentences: `我爱爸爸。` / `我爱妈妈。`.
- **1B Lesson 2 — Brothers and sisters:** Words: `哥哥、姐姐、弟弟、妹妹`. Task: identify family members.
- **2A Lesson 2 — Two younger brothers:** Words: `一个、两个、没有`. Frames: `我有…` / `我没有…。`.
- **3A Lesson 4 — Family:** `我家有五口人。爸爸、妈妈、哥哥、弟弟和我。` Words: `爷爷、奶奶、外公、外婆`. Task: introduce family members from a family photo.

## Result

The 2A sibling-count and ownership frame is now covered with the family-specific visual. Pets and toy ownership remain outside this page.
