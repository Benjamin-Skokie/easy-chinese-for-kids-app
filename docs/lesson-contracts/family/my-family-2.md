## Lesson evidence

- **4A Lesson 1 — Telephone numbers:** Words: `住、哪儿、他们、北京、电话号码、多少`. The book asks `你爷爷、奶奶住在哪儿？` and answers `他们住在北京。`; it also practises `电话号码是多少？` and notes that `一` is usually read `yāo` in telephone and room numbers.
