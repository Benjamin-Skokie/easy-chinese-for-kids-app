## Confirmed language and scope

The 3A nationality layer teaches `中国人、美国人、英国人、德国人、法国人`, with the question `你是哪国人？` and the answer frame `我是…人。` Its book practice asks learners to make a conversation for each picture. The 4A layer connects nationality and language through `…人说…语` and `我是…人。我说…语。`.

`assets/illustrated-country-symbols/` belongs to this existing Identity topic. Flags remain the primary country-recognition cue. A country symbol may support a scene or a secondary visual choice, but it must never imply that a symbol describes the people of that country.

The asset library also contains Canada, Mexico, and Australia. Keep those outside the first Book words, but they may appear as additional rounds in the flag-supported listening game. Germany is a 3A core word.

## Approved page architecture

Keep two focused sibling pages, because the later lesson has its own scene, Book Words, and practice:

1. **Nationality / 国籍** — 3A family-photo scene and picture conversation: `你是哪国人？` / `我是…人。`
2. **Languages / 语言** — 4A classmate scene and picture sentences: `…人说…语。`, followed by `我是…人。我说…语。`

Do not put one lesson behind a tab or a navigation shelf inside the other page.
