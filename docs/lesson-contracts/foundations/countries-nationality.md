## Confirmed language and scope

The 3A nationality layer teaches `中国`, `美国`, `英国`, and `法国`, with the question `你是哪国人？` and the answer frame `我是…人。` The 4A layer extends the same topic with language: `你会说…吗？` and `我会说…。`

`assets/illustrated-country-symbols/` belongs to this existing Identity topic. Flags remain the primary country-recognition cue. A country symbol may support a scene or a secondary visual choice, but it must never imply that a symbol describes the people of that country.

The asset library also contains Canada, Germany, Mexico, and Australia. They are not confirmed 3A nationality vocabulary, so do not add them to the first Book words or practice set merely because the art exists.

## Approved page architecture

Keep one **Countries & Languages** page, growing in two book layers:

1. **Where are you from?** — 3A countries, `你是哪国人？` and `我是…人。`
2. **What languages can you speak?** — 4A `你会说…吗？` and `我会说…。`

The page already owns its scenes and practice. Improve it in place rather than creating a separate country-symbol page.
