## Confirmed language and scope

`assets/illustrated-actions-basic/` contains `走`, `跑`, `坐`, `站`, `爬`, and `躺`. The books do not teach those six as one vocabulary set, so they must not become an invented Action Basics page.

Two items have confirmed child-facing lesson jobs:

- **4A Lesson 6:** `走路` in transport language, including `乐乐每天走路上学。`; `坐` means travel by in `坐…去…`, not the physical “sit” action shown by the current card.
- **4B Lesson 7:** `跑步` in leisure-time language, including `京京在公园里跑步。`

`站`, `爬`, and `躺` do not yet have verified book-backed uses as action vocabulary. Keep their cards available, but do not surface them until a specific lesson contract claims them.
