# Time & Daily Life Lesson Audit

Last updated: 2026-08-08

Purpose: lesson-contract audit for the current `时间和日常 / Time & Daily Life` category. It checks child-facing knowledge coverage, not whether an image asset exists and not whether the site repeats a textbook sentence exactly.

Scope: `weekdays`, `time-date`, `dates`, `comparisons`, `positions`, `transport`, and `daily-life`.

Status summary: 1 content-complete lesson, 9 partial lessons. The category has broad asset availability, but it is not content-complete.

## Required Coverage Standard

Each listed knowledge point needs evidence in at least one of these child-facing surfaces:

- scene or book-word card for vocabulary and a usable model sentence
- Useful Notes for grammar, alternatives, and distinctions
- practice that asks the child to recognize or build the pattern

Equivalent original sentences are acceptable. The lesson concept must still be explicit and usable.
