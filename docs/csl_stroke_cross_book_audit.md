# CSL cross-book writing audit

Status: curriculum scope confirmed from a page-by-page review of *Easy Steps to Chinese for Kids* 1A–4B. The textbooks use pinyin labels in the writing material; use pinyin as the learner-facing label, with Chinese and English as supporting labels.

## Scope rule

Do not infer that a stroke is taught merely because it appears inside a vocabulary word. The topic covers only the basic strokes and the named compound strokes / radicals explicitly encountered in the books.
