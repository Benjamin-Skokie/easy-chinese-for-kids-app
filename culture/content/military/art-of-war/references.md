## 《孙子兵法·谋攻篇》 (*The Art of War*, “Attack by Stratagem”)

Source: Chinese Text Project, *The Art of War*, “Attack by Stratagem”
URL: https://ctext.org/art-of-war/attack-by-stratagem/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-05
Supports: Contains the ideas that winning every battle is not the highest skill, that making an opponent submit without battle is preferable, and that attacking fortified cities is a costly, low-choice method.
Editorial boundary: “Without battle” remains part of an ancient military argument and is not rewritten as a claim that the opposing sides become friends.

## “知彼知己，百战不殆”

Source: Chinese Text Project, *The Art of War*, “Attack by Stratagem”
URL: https://ctext.org/art-of-war/attack-by-stratagem/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-05
Supports: Connects understanding the other side and oneself with avoiding danger across repeated battles.
Editorial boundary: The article explains *dài* through danger or peril and does not turn the line into a guarantee of automatic victory.

## 《孙子兵法·虚实篇》 (*The Art of War*, “Weak Points and Strong”)

Source: Chinese Text Project, *The Art of War*, “Weak Points and Strong”
URL: https://ctext.org/art-of-war/weak-points-and-strong/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-05
Supports: Uses water as the central image for adapting to changing conditions: “水因地而制流，兵因敌而制胜.”
Editorial boundary: The article keeps the metaphor focused on changing with conditions and does not add claims that water always avoids force or that one method applies everywhere.

## 银雀山汉墓竹简

Source: National Archives Administration of China / Shandong Museum, “Yinqueshan Han Tomb Bamboo Slips” nomination dossier
URL: https://www.saac.gov.cn/mowcn/cn/6sbs/202512/d58b9dd7d61e4344a7a5c0a1699011f6.shtml
Accessed: 2026-09-05
Supports: Documents the 1972 discovery at Yínquèshān, the nearly five thousand inscribed bamboo slips from Tomb No. 1, and the inclusion of an ancient copy of *The Art of War*.
Editorial boundary: The discovery establishes an early physical copy of the text; the article does not use it to settle disputes about every stage of the book’s authorship.
