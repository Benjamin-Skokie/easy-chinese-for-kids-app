[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一层一层往上长的塔
heading_en: A Tower That Grows Upward Floor by Floor
image: pagoda-odd-levels.webp
caption_zh: 宝塔一层比一层小，越往上看，越像在向天空爬。
caption_en: Each level of a pagoda is a little smaller than the one below, so it seems to climb toward the sky.
[zh]
宝塔是中国很有名的一种建筑。

它最容易认出来的地方，就是一层一层叠上去的屋檐。

从远处看，宝塔像一棵一直往上长的树，也像一支慢慢变细的毛笔。

有些宝塔不只是给人看，古人还会沿着楼梯一层一层往上走，看看远处的风景。
[/zh]
[en]
Pagodas are one of China's most famous kinds of buildings.

The easiest way to recognize one is by its stacked roofs, one level above another.

From far away, a pagoda can look like a tree growing upward or a brush that becomes thinner as it rises.

Some pagodas were not only meant to be admired from outside. Long ago, people could climb level by level and look out at the world.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 木头做的高塔，站了一千多年
heading_en: A Wooden Tower That Has Stood for More Than 1,000 Years
image: yingxian-wooden-pagoda.webp
caption_zh: 这座木塔很高很高，经历了风雨和地震，到今天还站着。
caption_en: This tall wooden pagoda has faced wind, rain, and earthquakes, and it is still standing today.
[zh]
山西有一座很有名的木塔，叫应县木塔。

它差不多有二十层楼那么高，却主要是木头做的。

最让人吃惊的是，它已经站了一千多年，还经历过很多风雨和地震。

这说明古代工匠不只是会盖漂亮房子，也很会想办法，让木头变得又高又稳。
[/zh]
[en]
In Shanxi, there is a famous wooden pagoda called the Yingxian Wooden Pagoda.

It is about as tall as a twenty-story building, yet it is made mainly of wood.

The most surprising part is that it has stood for more than a thousand years and has lived through storms and earthquakes.

That shows us something important: ancient builders did not only know how to make beautiful buildings. They also knew how to make wood strong and steady.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一座塔，也装着远方带回来的书
heading_en: A Tower That Also Held Books from Far Away
image: giant-wild-goose-pagoda.webp
caption_zh: 西安的大雁塔和远行归来的经书故事连在一起。
caption_en: The Giant Wild Goose Pagoda in Xi'an is tied to the story of sacred books brought home from a long journey.
[zh]
西安有一座很有名的宝塔，叫大雁塔。

人们常常会想到一个远行很久、后来带着书回来的和尚故事。

这些书从很远的地方带回中国，所以这座塔也像一个安静的大书架，把重要的东西好好保存起来。

这样一来，宝塔就不只是高高的建筑，也像在守护知识和故事。
[/zh]
[en]
In Xi'an, there is a famous pagoda called the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda.

It is closely tied to the story of a monk who traveled far away and later brought books back home.

Those books came from a distant land, so the pagoda became almost like a quiet giant bookshelf, keeping important things safe.

That means a pagoda was not only a tall building. It could also guard knowledge and stories.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 为什么宝塔常常是单数层？
heading_en: Why Do Pagodas Often Have Odd Numbers of Levels?
[zh]
如果你仔细看，会发现很多宝塔是三层、五层、七层，常常都是单数。

古人觉得这样的数字更适合往上长、往天空去的建筑。

而且宝塔一层一层变小，看起来特别整齐，也特别有节奏。

所以宝塔让人喜欢，不只是因为它高，还因为它像一首慢慢往上走的歌。
[/zh]
[en]
If you look carefully, many pagodas have three, five, or seven levels. Very often, the number is odd.

Long ago, people felt those numbers suited a building that rose upward toward the sky.

Also, the levels grow smaller one by one, which makes the whole tower feel neat and rhythmic.

That is why pagodas are so appealing. They are not only tall. They also look a little like a song rising upward.
[/en]
[/section]

heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
宝塔一层一层往上长，看起来像在接近天空。它可以让人登高看远，也可以保存书和故事。高高的外形里，藏着古代人对美、对安静、对远方的想象。
[/zh]
[en]
Pagodas rise level by level, as if they are reaching toward the sky. They can let people climb for a view, and they can also keep books and stories safe. Inside their tall shape lives an old dream of beauty, quiet, and faraway places.
[/en]
[/ending]
