heading_en: A Huge Place for Honoring the Sky

caption_zh: 蓝色的大殿高高立着，看起来像一把撑向天空的伞。
caption_en: The great blue hall rises high, looking almost like a giant umbrella opened toward the sky.
[zh]
天坛在北京，是皇帝举行重要仪式的地方。

这里最有名的建筑，是一座圆圆的蓝顶大殿，叫祈年殿。

它不是皇帝睡觉的地方，而是做大典的地方。

古代人觉得，天空、季节和收成都很重要，所以皇帝要到这里来，认真地举行仪式。
[/zh]
[en]
The Temple of Heaven is in Beijing. It was a place where emperors held very important ceremonies.

Its most famous building is a round blue-roofed hall called the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests.

This was not where the emperor slept. It was where great ceremonies happened.

Long ago, people felt that the sky, the seasons, and the harvest were very important, so the emperor came here to carry out these rituals with great care.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一面会传话的墙
heading_en: A Wall That Carries a Whisper
image: echo-wall.webp
caption_zh: 站在圆墙边轻声说话，声音会沿着墙面跑到远处去。
caption_en: If you whisper at the round wall, the sound can travel along it to someone far away.
[zh]
天坛里还有一个孩子们很喜欢的地方，叫回音壁。

它是一圈圆圆的墙。你贴着墙小声说话，声音会顺着墙面跑过去。

所以站在远一点的另一边的人，可能会听见你的声音。

这不是魔法，是工匠把墙修得很平整，又修成了圆形，声音才会这样走。
[/zh]
[en]
The Temple of Heaven also has a place many children love. It is called the Echo Wall.

It is a round wall. If you lean close and speak softly, your voice can travel along the wall.

That means a person standing far away on the other side may hear you.

This is not magic. It happens because the builders made the wall smooth and round, and sound moves across it in a special way.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3

heading_zh: 一圈又一圈的白石台
heading_en: Rings of White Stone

caption_zh: 圆形石台一层一层向外展开，让整个地方看起来安静又庄严。

[zh]
天坛还有一个圆形石台，叫圜丘坛。

它一圈又一圈展开，看起来很整齐，也很安静。

古代皇帝会站在中间，向上天读祭文。

这样的画面是在告诉大家：就算是皇帝，也要在天空和大自然面前低下头来。
[/zh]
[en]
The Temple of Heaven also has a round stone altar called the Circular Mound Altar.

It spreads outward in ring after ring. The shape feels neat, calm, and serious.

Long ago, the emperor stood in the middle and read a prayer upward to Heaven.

The scene sent a message: even an emperor had to bow his head before the sky and the natural world.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 没有钉子，也能站得很稳
heading_en: Strong Even Without Nails
[zh]
祈年殿还有一个很厉害的地方：它主要靠木头和木头咬在一起站稳。

这些木头拼得非常准，好像积木一样，一层托着一层。

所以大家常常会说，中国古建筑很聪明，不只是因为它好看，也因为它会“自己抱住自己”。

看上去安安静静的一座大殿，里面其实藏着很多工匠的脑筋。
[/zh]
[en]
The Hall of Prayer has another amazing feature: it stands mainly because wooden pieces lock into one another.

The parts fit together very carefully, almost like building blocks, one layer holding up the next.

That is why people often say old Chinese buildings were clever. They were not only beautiful. They also knew how to hold themselves together.

A quiet-looking hall can hide a great deal of smart thinking from the builders who made it.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
天坛不只是几座漂亮建筑。它也是一个让皇帝认真对待天空、季节和仪式的地方。蓝色的大殿、会传声的圆墙，还有一圈圈白石台，都让这里看起来特别安静又特别重要。
[/zh]
[en]
The Temple of Heaven was not just a group of beautiful buildings. It was also a place where the emperor had to take the sky, the seasons, and ceremony seriously. The blue hall, the whispering wall, and the white stone rings all make it feel calm and important.
[/en]
[/ending]
