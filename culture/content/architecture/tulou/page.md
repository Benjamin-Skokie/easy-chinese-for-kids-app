heading_zh: 一座楼，住下很多家
heading_en: One Building, Many Families
image: tulou-from-above.webp
caption_zh: 从上面看，土楼像一个大圆圈，把许多人的家抱在中间。
caption_en: Seen from above, a tulou looks like a giant ring holding many homes inside.
[zh]
福建山区里有一种很特别的房子，叫土楼。

很多土楼是圆圆的，从上面看，像一个大甜甜圈。

最特别的是，一座楼里常常可以住很多很多家人。

大家共用同一个院子、同一口井，也常常一起过节、一起吃饭。
[/zh]
[en]
In the mountains of Fujian, there is a very special kind of building called a tulou.

Many tulou are round. From above, they look like giant doughnuts.

The most special thing is this: one tulou could hold many families.

People shared one courtyard, one well, and often celebrated festivals and meals together.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 中间的院子像一个小世界
heading_en: The Courtyard Feels Like a Little World
image: tulou-courtyard.webp
caption_zh: 一圈圈木走廊围着中间的院子，开门就能见到邻居和亲戚。
caption_en: Round wooden balconies circle the courtyard, so neighbors and relatives are always close by.
[zh]
走进土楼里面，你会看到一圈一圈的木走廊和很多很多房门。

开门是亲戚，抬头是邻居。

小朋友在院子里跑，大人做饭、聊天、晒东西，整个地方像一个会呼吸的小村子。

虽然大家住在同一座楼里，可每一家也都有自己的房间和生活。
[/zh]
[en]
When you walk inside a tulou, you see rings of wooden balconies and many, many doors.

Open a door, and there is family. Look up, and there are neighbors.

Children can run in the courtyard while adults cook, talk, and dry things in the sun. The whole place feels like a little village that can breathe.

Even though everyone lives in one building, each family still has its own rooms and daily life.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 家，也是堡垒
heading_en: A Home That Was Also a Fortress
image: thick-wall-gate.webp
caption_zh: 厚厚的土墙、小小的高窗和结实的大门，让土楼也像一座堡垒。
caption_en: Thick earth walls, small high windows, and a strong gate made a tulou feel like a fortress too.
[zh]
土楼的墙很厚，有些厚得差不多像五英尺那么宽。

大门很结实，一楼常常没有窗，窗子开在更高的地方。

这是因为土楼不只是房子。有些时候，它还要保护里面的人。

如果外面不安全，大家把门一关，里面还是可以照常过日子。
[/zh]
[en]
Tulou walls are very thick. Some are close to five feet wide.

The main gate is strong. The first floor often has no windows, and the windows are higher up.

That is because a tulou was not only a home. Sometimes it also had to protect the people inside.

If the outside world was dangerous, families could close the gate and still keep living inside.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 为什么很多土楼都是圆的？
heading_en: Why Are So Many Tulou Round?
[zh]
圆形很好看，但它不只是为了好看。

圆圆的一圈，让大家更容易看到院子里的动静，也让整座楼看起来更稳。

更重要的是，圆形会让人觉得大家围在一起，像被一个大大的家抱住了。

所以土楼迷人的地方，不只是墙有多厚，也是不少人能把日子一起过在同一个圆圈里。
[/zh]
[en]
The round shape looks beautiful, but it was not only for beauty.

One big circle makes it easier to see what is happening in the courtyard, and it also helps the building feel steady.

Even more importantly, a circle can make people feel gathered together, almost as if one giant home is holding them.

That is why tulou are so fascinating. It is not only because the walls are thick. It is also because so many people could share one circle of daily life.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
土楼像房子，也像小村子，还像一座会保护人的堡垒。很多家人住在同一个圆圈里，让它看起来既温暖，又很有力量。
[/zh]
[en]
Tulou are like homes, little villages, and protective fortresses all at once. Many families lived inside one great circle, making these buildings feel warm and strong at the same time.
[/en]
