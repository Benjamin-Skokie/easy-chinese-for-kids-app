[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一走进寺庙，常常就先看见他在笑
heading_en: When You Enter a Temple, He Is Often the First One Smiling
image: 1-smiling-maitreya-statue.webp
caption_zh: 很多中国寺庙门口，都能看到笑呵呵、肚子圆圆的弥勒佛。
caption_en: At the entrance of many Chinese temples, you can see a smiling [[Maitreya]] with a round belly.
[zh]
在中国很多寺庙里，孩子一走进去，先看见的常常不是最严肃的佛像，而是一位笑呵呵的大肚子和尚。

他坐在那里，肚子圆圆的，脸也圆圆的，看起来一点都不凶。很多小朋友会忍不住多看两眼，甚至想伸手去摸一摸。

这就是中国人很熟悉的弥勒佛。

他让人一下放松下来，好像还没开口，就先在说：“别那么紧张，先笑一笑。”
[/zh]
[en]
In many Chinese temples, the first figure a child sees is not the most serious one. It is often a big-bellied monk with a warm smile.

He sits there with a round belly and a round face. He does not look frightening at all. Many children want to look again, and some even want to reach out and touch him.

This is the [[Maitreya]] many Chinese people know well.

He makes people relax at once, as if he is already saying, "Do not be so tense. Smile first."
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 后来，人们把他和一位爱笑的和尚连在了一起
heading_en: Later, People Linked Him with a Monk Who Loved to Laugh
image: 2-cloth-bag-monk.webp
caption_zh: 传说里的布袋和尚背着大布袋，走来走去，总是在笑。
caption_en: In legend, the [[Cloth-Bag Monk]] walks from place to place with a big sack and a constant smile.
[zh]
中国这个笑呵呵的弥勒佛，后来常常会让人想到一位很有名的和尚：布袋和尚。

传说里，他胖胖的，背着一个大布袋，到处走，到处笑。孩子跟着他，他笑；别人问他问题，他也笑。就算遇到麻烦，他还是笑。

大家慢慢觉得，这位和尚的样子和气质，和“将来会带来光明和快乐”的弥勒佛很像。

所以后来很多寺庙里的弥勒像，就越来越像这位爱笑、背着布袋四处走的和尚了。
[/zh]
[en]
Later, this smiling Chinese image of Maitreya became closely linked with a famous wandering monk: the [[Cloth-Bag Monk]].

In the stories, he is round and cheerful. He carries a large cloth sack and walks from place to place, always laughing. Children follow him, and he laughs. People ask him questions, and he laughs. Even when trouble comes, he still laughs.

Slowly, people began to feel that this monk's spirit matched Maitreya very well.

That is why many temple statues of Maitreya gradually came to look like this cheerful monk with the cloth bag.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 大肚子和笑脸，为什么会让人喜欢
heading_en: Why the Big Belly and the Smile Feel So Loved
[zh]
人们喜欢弥勒佛，不是因为他看起来很厉害，而是因为他看起来很能“装得下”。

装得下烦恼，装得下别人一时的坏脾气，也装得下生活里那些乱七八糟的事。

他的笑，也不是那种吵闹的笑。更像是在说：世界有时候就是会乱一点，可我们还是可以把心放宽一点。

所以很多人站在他面前，不管懂不懂佛教，都会觉得心里松了一点。
[/zh]
[en]
People love Maitreya not because he looks powerful, but because he seems able to hold a great deal.

He can hold worry, other people's bad moods, and the messy problems of everyday life.

His smile is not a noisy laugh. It feels more like this: the world can be confusing, but we can still make our hearts a little wider.

That is why many people, even those who do not know much about Buddhism, feel calmer when they stand before him.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
中国人很熟悉的弥勒佛，常常不是高高远远、让人不敢靠近的样子。

他更像一个会先对你笑一笑的人。大肚子、圆脸、布袋和笑容，让很多人一下就记住了他。
[/zh]
[en]
The Maitreya many Chinese people know does not feel far away or hard to approach.

He feels more like someone who smiles at you first. His round belly, round face, cloth bag, and easy smile help people remember him at once.
[/en]
[/ending]
