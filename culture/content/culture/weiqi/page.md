[section]
page: 1

heading_zh: 啪，一颗棋子落下去
heading_en: One Stone Lands, and the Whole Board Changes
image: 1-first-stones-board.webp
caption_zh: 棋盘上明明只有几颗子，可下棋的人已经在想整盘棋会怎么走。
caption_en: Even when only a few stones are on the board, the players are already thinking about the whole game.
[zh]
啪。一颗黑子落在木棋盘上。对面的人看看，又轻轻放下一颗白子。再一颗黑子，再一颗白子。房间里很安静，最清楚的声音，就是棋子落到棋盘上的那一下。

站在旁边的人也许会觉得：不就是放下一颗小石子吗？可下棋的人看到的，常常不是眼前这一小块，而是整张棋盘以后会怎么变。

这就是围棋最神奇的地方。棋子不大，动作也不吵，可一颗子落下去，整盘棋的样子已经开始改变了。
[/zh]
[en]
Tap. A black stone lands on the wooden board. The other player looks for a moment and places a white stone beside it. Then black again. Then white again. The room is quiet, and the clearest sound is each stone touching the board.

Someone watching from the side may think, "It is only one little stone." But the players are usually seeing much more than that tiny corner. They are already thinking about how the whole board may change later.

That is part of the magic of weiqi. The stones are small, and the moves are quiet. Still, each move can begin changing the shape of the whole game.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 规则不算难，难的是整盘都要看
heading_en: The Rules Are Simple, but the Whole Board Is Hard
image: 2-weiqi-board-closeup-reference-style-v3.webp
caption_zh: 黑子先下，白子后下，围住对方、围出地盘，规则不多，可变化非常多。
caption_en: Black goes first, white goes second, and players fight for territory. The rules are short, but the possibilities are huge.
[zh]
围棋的基本规则并不算难。黑子先下，白子后下。棋子放在交叉点上，通常就不再移动。把对方围住，可以把对方提掉；最后再看谁围出的地盘更大。

听起来很简单，对吧？可真正难的地方，是你不能只盯着眼前一块。你在左上角下的一手，可能会慢慢影响到右下角。你今天局部吃点亏，明天全局反而赚回来。

所以很多人学围棋的时候，最先学到的不是“怎么马上赢”，而是“怎么把整盘都放进眼睛里”。
[/zh]
[en]
The basic rules of weiqi are not very hard to learn. Black goes first. White goes second. Stones are placed on the crossing points and usually stay there. If you surround the other side, you can capture stones. At the end, players count who built more territory.

That sounds simple enough. The hard part is that you cannot stare at only one small area. A move in the upper left may slowly matter in the lower right. You may lose a little in one place and gain more later somewhere else.

That is why many people say the first lesson in weiqi is not, "How do I win right away?" The first lesson is, "How do I learn to see the whole board?"
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 2016年，很多不下围棋的人也盯住了棋盘
heading_en: In 2016, Even Nonplayers Started Watching
[zh]
2016年，人工智能 AlphaGo 和韩国顶尖棋手李世石下了一场很有名的比赛。很多本来不下围棋的人，也第一次认真看起了棋盘。

最让人吃惊的，不只是 AlphaGo 赢了四盘，而是它有一步棋下得和职业棋手平常熟悉的路子很不一样。很多人一下子意识到：原来围棋这盘古老的游戏，里面还藏着人类没有走惯的新路。

这场比赛让更多人发现，围棋不是只有“古老”两个字。它还一直活着，一直能让人重新思考“聪明”到底是什么样子。
[/zh]
[en]
In 2016, the computer program AlphaGo played a famous match against the top Korean player Lee Sedol. Many people who did not usually play weiqi began paying close attention to the board for the first time.

What surprised people was not only that AlphaGo won four games. It was also that one of its moves looked very different from the path professional players were used to seeing. Suddenly, many people felt that this ancient game still had new roads hidden inside it.

The match reminded the world that weiqi is not interesting only because it is old. It is also interesting because it keeps making people think again about what smart thinking can look like.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 围棋像一面很安静的镜子
heading_en: Weiqi Can Feel Like a Quiet Mirror
[zh]
围棋不只看你吃了几颗子，也看你最后围出了多少地盘。有时候你眼前好像输了，可整盘反而变好了。很多人喜欢从这里读出一种想法：别只盯着面前这一小块，要看更大的局面。

还有一点很特别，一手棋落下去，通常就不能反悔。你得对自己的选择负责。下完以后回头复盘，也得老老实实承认：到底是哪一步开始走偏了。围棋不吵，也不闹，可它很会把人的急躁、犹豫和耐心照出来。
[/zh]
[en]
Weiqi is not only about how many stones you capture. It is also about how much space you finally build. Sometimes a position looks bad right in front of you, yet the whole board may quietly be turning in your favor. That is one reason people often say weiqi teaches you to look past the nearest corner and notice the larger picture.

Another special thing is that you usually cannot take a move back. Once the stone lands, you must live with that choice. Later, when players review the game, they have to look honestly at where things started to go wrong. Weiqi does not shout, but it shows a great deal. It can reveal your impatience, your doubt, and your patience all at once.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
围棋的规则不算多，可一颗一颗下下去，变化就会多得像看不见的风。它安静、古老，又总是新的。

所以很多人喜欢围棋，不只是因为它考脑子，也因为它在提醒人：想看清一件事，有时候得先学会把整盘都看进去。
[/zh]
[en]
Weiqi does not have many basic rules, but once the stones keep landing, the changes multiply like invisible wind. It is quiet, ancient, and somehow always new.

That is why so many people love it. Not only because it tests the mind, but because it keeps reminding us that to understand one part well, we sometimes have to learn to see the whole board.
[/en]
[/ending]
