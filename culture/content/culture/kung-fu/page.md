[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 功夫不只是打架，更像“下功夫”
heading_en: Kung Fu Means More Than Fighting
image: 1-kung-fu-means-practice.webp
caption_zh: 真正的功夫，不只是飞踢和翻滚，更是一天一天练出来的本事。
caption_en: Real kung fu is not only kicks and flips. It is skill built through patient practice.
[zh]
一提到功夫，很多孩子先想到的是电影：飞踢、翻滚、双节棍，动作快得眼睛都跟不上。

可“功夫”这个词，原来的意思不只是打架。它更像“下功夫”这三个字的意思：你在一件事上花了很多时间、很多心力，慢慢练出了真本事，这就叫有功夫。

所以，厨师切菜切得特别好，可以说有功夫；写毛笔字的人，笔一落下去就很稳，也可以说有功夫。武术当然也是功夫，但它不是这个词唯一的意思。
[/zh]
[en]
When many children hear the words Chinese kung fu, they think first of movies: flying kicks, flips, and fast-moving action.

But the word kung fu did not begin as a word that meant only fighting. It also means the skill you build by giving something a great deal of time, effort, and practice. If you work at something patiently for years, people can say you have real kung fu in it.

That means a cook can have kung fu with a knife. A calligrapher can have kung fu with a brush. A martial artist can have kung fu with movement and control. Fighting is only one part of the story.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 说到功夫，很多人会想到少林
heading_en: Many People Think First of Shaolin
image: 2-shaolin-training-yard.webp
caption_zh: 少林寺常常被看作中国功夫最有名的一个源头。
caption_en: Shaolin is often remembered as one of the most famous homes of Chinese martial arts.
[zh]
说到中国功夫，很多人都会想到少林寺。它在河南嵩山，和武术故事连在一起已经很久很久了。

关于少林武术的起源，有很多传说。有人会讲到达摩和僧人练身体的故事，也有人会讲后来很多武术高手到了少林，把自己的本事带进去。哪一部分是传说，哪一部分是历史，常常混在一起。

可有一点很清楚：少林后来成了很多人心里“功夫”的代表。少林拳、棍法、练功的院子、清晨的号声，这些画面一出现，很多人立刻就会想到中国武术。
[/zh]
[en]
When people talk about Chinese martial arts, many of them think first of Shaolin Temple. It stands on Mount Song in Henan, and stories about kung fu have been tied to it for a very long time.

There are many legends about how Shaolin martial arts began. Some tell of Bodhidharma and monks strengthening their bodies. Others tell of skilled fighters who later entered Shaolin and brought their own training with them. Story and history are often mixed together here.

But one thing is clear: Shaolin became one of the best-known faces of kung fu. Shaolin boxing, staff training, temple courtyards, and early morning practice all became pictures that many people connect right away with Chinese martial arts.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 中国这么大，打法当然不会都一样
heading_en: A Big Country Grew Many Different Styles
[zh]
中国这么大，同样叫功夫，不同地方的打法当然不会都一样。老话说“南拳北腿”，意思是南方很多拳法更重手上的快和近，北方很多拳法更重腿上的伸展和力量。这个说法不可能把所有门派都装进去，可它提醒你：功夫不是一种动作包打天下，而是长出了很多不同的路子。

后来人们还常把一些路子分成“外家”和“内家”。少林常被看成刚猛、快速的一边；太极常被看成柔和、缓慢的一边。可“慢”不等于没力量，“快”也不等于乱冲。真正厉害的人，往往最知道什么时候该进，什么时候该退，什么时候把力气收住。
[/zh]
[en]
China is huge, so of course kung fu did not grow in only one style. There is an old saying, "southern fists, northern legs." It means many southern styles pay more attention to close hand work, while many northern styles are famous for longer kicks and big leg movement. The saying does not cover every school, but it reminds you that kung fu spread into many different paths.

People later also talked about harder outward styles and softer inward styles. Shaolin is often used as an example of the strong, fast side. Taiji is often used as an example of the softer, slower-looking side. But slow does not mean weak, and fast does not mean wild. The most skillful people often know exactly when to move forward, when to step back, and when to hold their power in reserve.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 看起来最慢的动作，里面也可能藏着劲
heading_en: Even Slow Movements Can Hide Real Power
image: 3-taiji-park.webp
caption_zh: 公园里慢慢打太极的人，看起来安静，可他们练的也是控制和力量。
caption_en: People practicing taiji in the park may move slowly, but they are still training balance, control, and power.
[zh]
很多人第一次看到太极，会觉得：这不是像在慢动作里练拳吗？

可太极最有意思的地方，就在这里。它看起来慢，是因为动作被放稳了，被拉长了，让你能把呼吸、平衡和用力都放进去。慢，不代表没劲；慢，反而可能是在练一种更难的控制。

这也是为什么很多人说，功夫不只是练身体，也是在练心。你急，动作就散；你乱，脚下就不稳。看起来最安静的时候，里面反而最考验人。
[/zh]
[en]
The first time many people see taiji, they may think, "Isn't this just very slow fighting?"

But that is exactly what makes it interesting. The movement looks slow because it has been stretched out and steadied, so breathing, balance, and force can all be placed into it. Slow does not mean powerless. Slow can mean a deeper kind of control.

That is one reason people say kung fu trains more than muscles. It also trains the mind. If you are rushed, your movement falls apart. If you are distracted, your feet become unsteady. The calmest-looking moment may be the one that asks the most from you.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 5
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 真正吸引人的，不只是电影，是里面那种练出来的分寸
heading_en: The Real Attraction Is the Discipline Inside It
[zh]
功夫电影当然让全世界很多人先注意到了中国武术。李小龙、成龙这些名字，让很多孩子第一次觉得：原来动作也可以像说故事一样有节奏、有个性。

可功夫真正一直吸引人的地方，不只是好看，而是它背后那种“练出来”的味道。你要反复练，要学会收住自己，要知道能不打就不打，要把力量放在该放的地方。功夫里常常讲尊重、节制、坚持，这些东西比赢一场架更难，也更重要。
[/zh]
[en]
Kung fu movies helped people around the world notice Chinese martial arts. Names like Bruce Lee and Jackie Chan showed many children that movement itself could tell a story.

But the deeper reason kung fu keeps attracting people is not only that it looks exciting. It is the feeling of discipline inside it. You repeat movements, control yourself, and learn that avoiding a fight can be stronger than starting one. Kung fu keeps pointing back to respect, restraint, and steady practice. Those things are harder than winning one quick fight, and they matter more.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
功夫不只是电影里的飞踢，也不只是拳头和兵器。它更像一种长期练出来的本事：少林有少林的路，太极有太极的路，快有快的劲，慢也有慢的劲。

所以很多人喜欢功夫，不只是因为它看起来厉害，更因为它在提醒人：真正稳的力量，往往是慢慢练出来的。
[/zh]
[en]
Kung fu is not only movie kicks, and it is not only fists and weapons. It is a long-built skill. Shaolin has its way. Taiji has its way. Fast movement has its power, and slow movement has its power too.

That is why so many people love kung fu. Not only because it looks impressive, but because it keeps reminding us that the steadiest strength is usually built little by little.
[/en]
[/ending]
