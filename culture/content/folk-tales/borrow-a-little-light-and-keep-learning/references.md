# Editorial References: Borrow a Little Light and Keep Learning

## 《西京杂记》“匡衡勤学”

Source: Chinese Text Project, *Xijing Zaji*
URL: https://ctext.org/xijing-zaji/zhs
Accessed: 2026-09-05

Supports:

- The classical account identifies the student as Kuang Heng and says that, because he had no candle, he opened the wall to use light from a neighboring home while reading.
- The account establishes the small beam of borrowed light and determined study at the center of the story.

Editorial boundary:

- The child-facing story omits the longer episode about Kuang Heng working in exchange for access to books.
- It retells the scene as a story about continuing to learn rather than making a claim about a universal route to success.
- The picture is a modern storybook illustration, not a historical reconstruction.
