# Legend of the White Snake: A Magical Snake Sister

## Meeting on Broken Bridge: One Umbrella and a Thousand-Year Bond

Long, long ago, on Broken Bridge by West Lake in Hangzhou, a young student named Xu Xian met two women.

One wore white. One wore green.

Suddenly, rain began to fall.

The woman in white had no umbrella, so Xu Xian gave her his.

She smiled softly and said, "Kind sir, I will return your umbrella another day."

In the story, this woman in white is really a great white snake who has practiced magic for 1,800 years.

Her name is Bai Suzhen.

The girl in green is Xiaoqing, a green snake who has also taken human shape.

Bai Suzhen has not come to hurt anyone.

She has come to repay kindness.

Long ago, in a past life, Xu Xian was said to be a shepherd boy. He once bought an injured little white snake from a snake catcher and set it free.

That little white snake later became Bai Suzhen.

---

## Dragon Boat Festival: Xu Xian Is Frightened to Death

Bai Suzhen marries Xu Xian, and the two open a medicine shop.

They help people who are sick, and their days are peaceful.

Neighbors say Xu Xian has married a wife as kind and skilled as a fairy.

Then comes Dragon Boat Festival.

On this day, people had a custom of drinking wine mixed with *realgar*, a yellow stone-like material believed to drive away snakes and harmful things.

Bai Suzhen knows she must not drink it.

If a snake spirit touches realgar wine, she may show her true shape.

But she cannot bear to refuse Xu Xian, so she takes a tiny sip.

Late that night, Xu Xian carries a small meal into the room.

Where his wife should be, he sees a huge white snake coiled on the bed, her shining scales glowing faintly.

Xu Xian is so frightened that his heart cannot bear it.

He falls to the ground and dies.

Bai Suzhen wakes from the wine and sees her husband's body.

She cries for a long time.

Then she makes a decision:

she will save this human man, no matter what.

---

## Stealing the Magic Herb: Fighting for Xu Xian

Bai Suzhen flies to Kunlun Mountain to ask for a magic herb.

In the story, this herb can bring Xu Xian back to life.

At first, the magical old sage of the south does not want to give it to her.

Bai Suzhen begs and begs. Later, she even fights the young guardians of the mountain.

Blood stains her white clothes.

Seeing how far she is willing to go for Xu Xian, the magical old sage finally softens.

Bai Suzhen carries the lingzhi herb back to Hangzhou and boils it into medicine.

She pours it into Xu Xian's mouth.

Xu Xian wakes up.

He sees his wife kneeling beside the bed with the medicine bowl.

He remembers everything: the great snake on the bed, his wife trembling as she drank the wine, and Bai Suzhen flying through danger to bring back the herb.

He holds her hand.

He says nothing.

---

## Flooding Jinshan: She Will Not Give Up

The monk Fahai finds Xu Xian.

He tells him, "Your wife is a snake spirit."

Then he keeps Xu Xian inside Jinshan Temple and will not let him leave.

Bai Suzhen and Xiaoqing come to the temple to ask for him back.

They kneel outside the gate for a day and a night.

Fahai stands behind the wooden door and says nothing.

At last, Bai Suzhen stands up and faces the river.

She and Xiaoqing use magic, and floodwater rushes toward Jinshan Temple.

Fahai uses his own magic tools to hold the water back.

Both sides push and push.

Bai Suzhen is using all her strength. She only wants to bring Xu Xian home.

But then pain strikes her belly.

She is carrying Xu Xian's child.

Her magic weakens.

Different versions tell the next part in different ways. In some, Xu Xian escapes and holds her. In others, Fahai still captures Bai Suzhen.

In the most common version, Bai Suzhen is trapped under Leifeng Pagoda in Hangzhou.

---

## After Leifeng Pagoda Falls

Bai Suzhen stays under Leifeng Pagoda for many years.

In the story, her son Xu Shilin grows up and passes the big government exams.

He comes to the pagoda to honor his mother.

Then the pagoda falls, and Bai Suzhen finally sees daylight again.

In real life, Leifeng Pagoda really did fall in 1924.

Because of that, people began to connect the legend and the real place in their minds.

---

## Summary

In the story of the White Snake, Bai Suzhen does astonishing things for kindness and love.

She steals the magic herb, floods Jinshan Temple, and is trapped under Leifeng Pagoda.

Different versions tell the details in different ways.

But one feeling stays the same:

once Bai Suzhen gives her heart, she is willing to fight to the end.

That is why this story has been told again and again.
