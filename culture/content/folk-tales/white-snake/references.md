# Editorial References: The Legend of the White Snake

## 1. A story built over many centuries

Source: China Intangible Cultural Heritage, “The Legend of the White Snake”
URL: https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/12185.html

- Lists the legend as national intangible cultural heritage in the folk-literature category, with Hangzhou as the submitting region.
- Names Bai Suzhen, Xu Xian, Fahai, and Xiaoqing as the tale's major figures.
- Explains that the legend began to develop in the Tang and Five Dynasties periods, took clearer shape in the Southern Song, and continued changing through oral stories, drama, novels, film, television, and other art forms.
- Identifies Feng Menglong's Ming-dynasty “White Lady Forever Trapped beneath Leifeng Pagoda” as the earliest relatively complete surviving text, not as the only form of the tale.

Editorial boundary:

- The child-facing article explicitly follows one familiar modern story line rather than claiming that every episode belonged to one unchanged ancient original.
- Modern character names and sympathetic relationships reflect later retellings.

## 2. Why Bai Suzhen comes to West Lake

Source: Hangzhou CPPCC News, “Do You Remember *New Legend of Madame White Snake*? The Classic Will Be Remade”
URL: https://www.hzzx.gov.cn/cshz/content/2016-05/12/content_6172784_0.htm

- Summarizes the popular repayment version in which Bai Suzhen meets Xu Xian at Broken Bridge to repay a kindness from an earlier life.
- Connects the best-known episodes with West Lake, the umbrella meeting, Fahai, the magic herb, Jinshan Temple, and Xiaoqing.

Source: Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the People's Republic of China, National Centre for the Performing Arts production of *The Legend of the White Snake*
URL: https://www.mct.gov.cn/ggfw/zyjzzt/yanchu/yanchuwqhg/202411/t20241107_956108.html

- Uses another familiar motivation: Bai Suzhen and Xiaoqing take human form and come from Mount Emei to the human world in search of love.
- Includes the medicine shop, realgar wine, Xu Xian's collapse, Bai Suzhen's journey for a life-saving herb, Fahai and Jinshan Temple, Xiaoqing's continuing role, and the couple's later reunion.

Editorial boundary:

- The article selects the repayment-of-kindness opening because it gives a young child a clear answer to “Why did she come to West Lake?”
- The references preserve the fact that other respected versions say she came because she was curious about human life or was looking for love.
- The brief umbrella expressions and Xiaoqing's spoken line are new storybook connective details, not quotations from a traditional script.

### What realgar wine was

Source: Beijing Municipal Health Commission, Beijing Huairou Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine, “Do Not Drink Realgar Wine at Dragon Boat Festival”
URL: https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jksh/202606/t20260618_4707065.html

- Explains that realgar is an arsenic-containing mineral and that drinking realgar wine was an old Dragon Boat Festival custom based on the belief that it could repel harmful creatures and illness.
- Warns that realgar is poisonous and should not be swallowed, especially by children.

Editorial boundary:

- The child-facing story keeps the definition general: realgar wine is introduced only as a special festival wine that people once believed could keep snakes and insects away.
- The more detailed composition and modern safety information remain in this internal editorial reference rather than interrupting the story.

## 3. Xiaoqing, Leifeng Pagoda, and the rescue ending

Source: Beijing Municipal Government, Chang'an Grand Theatre, Peking Opera *The Legend of the White Snake*
URL: https://www.beijing.gov.cn/gate/big5/www.beijing.gov.cn/renwen/whrl/rdtj/201905/t20190520_1871303.html

- Presents Bai Suzhen and Xiaoqing arriving together at West Lake, the umbrella meeting, realgar wine, the life-saving herb, Jinshan Temple, and Bai Suzhen being trapped beneath Leifeng Pagoda.
- Uses the stage ending in which Xiaoqing survives, returns with help, defeats the tower's guards, and frees Bai Suzhen.

Editorial boundary:

- Other versions end with Bai Suzhen still beneath the tower, with her son freeing her, or with a different form of reunion.
- This article uses Xiaoqing's rescue because the existing final illustration clearly shows Xiaoqing waiting outside the tower and because it completes her role as Bai Suzhen's loyal companion.
- The flood and battle are kept brief. The child-facing article does not describe injuries or deaths.

## 4. Place note

Source: Sichuan Provincial People's Congress, Xinhua report, “Savoring the Scenery beside West Lake”
URL: https://www.scspc.gov.cn/html/xxyl_66/lyyl_68/2010/0524/54378.html

- Identifies West Lake as a real scenic area in Hangzhou and connects Broken Bridge and Leifeng Pagoda with the long popularity of the White Snake legend.
