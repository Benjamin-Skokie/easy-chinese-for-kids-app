# The Old Man Who Lost His Horse

## The Horse Ran Away

Long ago, an old man lived with his son near China’s northern border. The family owned a horse, and one day, it suddenly ran away. The neighbors came to comfort the old man. “What bad luck!” they said. But the old man did not seem too upset. “Perhaps,” he said. “But who knows? This may turn out to be something good.”

---

## The Horse Came Home with Another Horse

A few months later, the missing horse came home. And it was not alone—it brought another fine horse with it. The neighbors hurried over again. “What wonderful luck!” But the old man only smiled. “Perhaps. But who knows? This may turn out to be something bad.” Not long afterward, the old man’s son was riding one of the horses when he fell and badly injured his leg. Once again, the neighbors came. “What terrible luck!” And once again, the old man said, “Perhaps. But who knows? This may turn out to be something good.” About a year later, fighting reached the border. Young men in the area were called away to fight. But the old man’s son could not go because his leg had not healed. The injury that had seemed like such bad luck kept him safely at home. This old story gave us the expression **sài wēng shī mǎ**. It reminds us that we cannot always tell right away whether something will turn out to be good or bad. A lost horse may come home with another horse. Good luck may bring trouble. And bad luck may lead somewhere we never expected. Sometimes, we simply have to wait and see.
