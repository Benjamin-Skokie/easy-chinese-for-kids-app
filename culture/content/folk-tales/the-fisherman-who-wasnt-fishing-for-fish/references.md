Source: Chinese Text Project, *Records of the Grand Historian*, “Genealogy of Tai Gongwang of Qi”
URL: https://ctext.org/shiji/qi-tai-gong-shi-jia/zhs
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- 《史记》记载吕尚年老时曾以钓鱼接近周西伯，后来在渭水之阳与周西伯相遇。
- 周西伯与吕尚交谈后非常高兴，把他一同带回并立为师。

Editorial boundary:

- 姜子牙、姜太公和吕尚均指故事中的同一人物。
- “直钩、无饵、愿者上钩”属于后来广泛流传的民间故事版本，并不是《史记》原文中的细节。
- The dialogue, passersby, and explanations of Jiang Ziya’s thoughts are child-facing narrative adaptation rather than quotations from the historical text.
- The picture is a modern storybook illustration, not a historical reconstruction.
