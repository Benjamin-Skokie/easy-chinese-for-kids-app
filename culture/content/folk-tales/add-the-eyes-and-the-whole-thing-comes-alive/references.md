## Zhang Sengyao and “Adding Eyes to the Dragon”

Source: Shanghai Municipal Government, “Idioms for the Year of the Dragon (II)”
URL: https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-LearnChinese/20240320/c217ac1db3824f1496c96f1a3863a248.html
Accessed: 2026-09-05

Supports:

- The account attributes the story to Zhang Yanyuan’s *History of Famous Paintings Across Dynasties* and identifies the painter as Zhang Sengyao.
- It recounts that the painter left the dragons’ eyes unfinished, warned that adding them would make the dragons fly away, and then saw the completed dragons come alive and depart.

Editorial boundary:

- The page presents the event as a legend, not as literal historical fact.
- The child-facing version omits names, dates, and the number of dragons because its focus is the self-contained story supplied in the original page.
- The picture is a modern storybook illustration, not a historical reconstruction.
