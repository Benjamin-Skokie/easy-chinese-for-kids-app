## Add the Eyes, and the Whole Thing Comes Alive

In the story of “adding eyes to the dragon,” a painter finished the whole dragon but left out the eyes. Other people were puzzled, but he said that once the eyes were added, the dragon would fly away. Then he made the final stroke. In the legend, the dragon suddenly came alive. You do not have to believe the legend literally to understand why people kept the story. Sometimes a great deal of work is already finished, and then one final important step suddenly makes the whole thing shine. That is the power remembered by “adding eyes to the dragon.”
