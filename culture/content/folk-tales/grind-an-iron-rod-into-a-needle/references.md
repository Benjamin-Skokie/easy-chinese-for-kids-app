## 《方舆胜览·眉州》“铁杵成针”

Source: National Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools, unified Chinese-language textbook
URL: https://v1.ykt.cbern.com.cn/65/document/63515014739442d39834a8dab7b02c2c/pdf.pdf
Accessed: 2026-09-05

Supports:

- The textbook identifies the passage as coming from Zhu Mu’s *Fangyu Shenglan*, “Meizhou.”
- The passage recounts that Li Bai left his studies, met an old woman grinding an iron rod into a needle by a stream, and returned to finish his studies after understanding her determination.

Editorial boundary:

- The source presents this as a story handed down about Li Bai, not as an independently verified event from his childhood.
- The child-facing version keeps the traditional sequence and gives its familiar lesson without adding further biographical claims.
- The picture is a modern storybook illustration, not a historical reconstruction.
