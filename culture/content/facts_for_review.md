## Terminology Review — Author Response

### Overuse of `华夏先民`

- ✅ **Agree strongly.** This is the single best critique in the document. `华夏先民` most naturally refers to early ancestral populations, not Tang/Song/Ming/Qing people. Current usage is too broad.

**Fix plan:**
- Keep `华夏先民` for: pre-Qin, Han and earlier, truly ancient ancestral references
- Revert to `华夏先民` / `中国工匠` / `中国文人` for: Tang through Qing
- Use specific terms where possible: `唐朝人` `宋朝读书人` `明朝工匠`

### `中国` used where period label better

- ✅ **Agree.** In historical contexts, prefer `古代中国` or a specific dynasty name over bare `中国`.

### `中文` vs `汉字` vs `Chinese` confusion in family-titles

- ✅ **Agree.** Will clarify in English translation.

### Family-titles article uses `华夏先民` too broadly

- ✅ **Agree.** See terminology fix plan above.
