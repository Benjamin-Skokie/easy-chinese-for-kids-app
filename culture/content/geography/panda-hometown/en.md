# The Giant Panda's Home: Sichuan's Green Mountains and Clear Water

## A Bear That Almost Only Eats Bamboo

The giant panda is a very picky eater. It mostly eats bamboo, and not just any bamboo. In the mountains of Sichuan, there are more than 200 kinds of bamboo. Pandas eat only about a dozen of them.

A panda may eat for 12 to 16 hours a day. It can eat more than 30 kilograms of bamboo, about as heavy as a 10-year-old child. Bamboo does not give much energy, so pandas have to keep eating and eating.

But here is the funny part: a panda's stomach is not built like a grass-eating animal's stomach. It is built more like a bear's. Long ago, panda ancestors ate meat. Their stomach and intestines are short and not very good at breaking down tough bamboo.

So a lot of the bamboo goes through without giving the panda much energy. That is one reason pandas always seem to be eating. They eat a lot, but their bodies do not get a lot from each bite.

## Sichuan: Why Do Pandas Live There?

Wild giant pandas live in a small area. Most live in high mountain forests where Sichuan, Shaanxi, and Gansu meet.

These mountains are cool and wet. They are also full of the arrow bamboo pandas like to eat.

Long ago, pandas lived across a wider area. After the Ice Age, the weather changed over a long time. People also spread into more land. Little by little, pandas moved back into deep mountain forests. Today there are about 1,800 wild giant pandas, and most of them live in mountain bamboo forests.

## Why Do So Many People Love Pandas?

First, look at them. Round body. Black-and-white fur. Big black eye patches that make them look as if their eyes are wide open and innocent.

Then watch how they move. A panda does not always look like a powerful bear. It sits down to chew bamboo. It holds a bamboo stem in both front paws, almost like a child holding corn. It chews, tilts its head, and somehow makes people laugh just by eating lunch.

In Chinese, people sometimes call pandas `gun gun`, a cute nickname that means something like "rolly-rolly." The name comes from the way pandas can tumble and roll, especially on a slope.

Pandas have also become famous around the world. Some zoos wait many years to receive pandas from China. When two pandas arrived in the Netherlands after a long wait, people treated their arrival like a big national event. Belgium even built a Chinese-style home for its pandas. This is why people sometimes talk about `panda diplomacy`, which means using pandas to help countries feel friendly toward each other. A black-and-white bear became a very popular friendship star.

## Wild or Raised by People?

The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding is one of the world's best-known places for caring for pandas born with help from people.

A newborn panda cub is tiny, only about 100 grams. That is about the weight of a pen in your hand. It is pink, almost hairless, and very weak. Caregivers keep the cubs warm and feed them special milk every few hours.

When the cubs grow bigger and turn black and white, they become the little rolling stars people love to watch.

But raising pandas is not the final goal. In Sichuan, people are also working to return some pandas to the wild. That is very hard. A panda may need years of training before it can live on its own. It must learn to avoid people and dogs, find bamboo, and find water.

When it works, a panda can go back to the bamboo forests where its ancestors lived for millions of years.

---

## Summary

The giant panda's home is in the high mountain bamboo forests of Sichuan and nearby areas. It mostly eats bamboo, but its body still works more like a meat-eating bear's body, so it spends many hours every day eating. Today, people protect wild bamboo forests and also help some pandas born with help from people return to the forest. That way, this black-and-white mountain neighbor can keep living in its real home.
