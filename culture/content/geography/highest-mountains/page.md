[section]

heading_zh: 珠穆朗玛——离天空最近的地方之一
heading_en: Qomolangma: One of the Closest Places to the Sky
image: qomolangma-location-map.png
caption_zh: 珠穆朗玛峰在喜马拉雅山脉里，立在中国和尼泊尔的交界处
caption_en: Qomolangma stands in the Himalayas on the border between China and Nepal.
[zh]
地球上海拔最高的山峰在哪里？不在欧洲，不在非洲，也不在美洲，而是在亚洲——中国和尼泊尔的边界上，矗立着一座八千八百多米的山峰。珠穆朗玛峰。西方人常叫它Everest。

如果把许多很高的摩天大楼叠在一起，珠峰还是会高得惊人。它现在还在悄悄变化——印度板块一直在挤向亚欧板块，喜马拉雅山脉也因此缓慢抬升。你手上的指甲一年能长三十毫米，山脉一年可能只变一点点，可把时间放大到几千万年，这股力量就能把古老的海底慢慢顶成高山。
[/zh]
[en]
Where is the highest mountain on Earth? It is not in Europe, Africa, or the Americas. It is in Asia, on the border between China and Nepal.

The mountain is more than 8,800 meters high. In Chinese, it is called Qomolangma. Many English speakers call it Mount Everest.

Try imagining a stack of very tall skyscrapers. Even then, Everest would still feel shockingly high.

And the mountain is still changing, very slowly. The Indian Plate keeps pushing into the Eurasian Plate. That push helps lift the Himalayas. Your fingernails may grow about 30 millimeters in a year. A mountain may rise only a tiny bit in that time. But give the Earth millions of years, and that slow push can lift an ancient sea floor into high mountains.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 会游动的雪线
heading_en: A Snow Line That Seems to Move
image: everest-snowline-vertical-climate.webp
caption_zh: 从冰雪山顶到绿色山谷，像从北极走向赤道
caption_en: From icy peak to green valley, the mountain changes like a trip across climates.
[zh]
珠峰顶部终年覆盖着冰雪，气温长年在零下三十度以下，狂风几无停歇。但奇妙的是，在山脚下——海拔三四千米的喜马拉雅南坡——却是郁郁葱葱的森林和花海。世界最高峰从雪山顶到热带山谷，垂直高度差超过六七千米，等于从北极走到了赤道。
[/zh]
[en]
The top of Everest is covered with ice and snow all year. The air is freezing cold, often far below zero. Strong winds blow again and again.

But near the foot of the mountain, on the southern side of the Himalayas, the world can look completely different. At around 3,000 or 4,000 meters high, there can be green forests and flowers.

That is one amazing thing about the world's highest mountains. From snowy peaks down to warm valleys, the change in height can be more than 6,000 or 7,000 meters. It is almost like traveling from the Arctic toward the equator, but going up and down instead of north and south.
[/en]
[/section]

heading_en: Who Does Everest Belong To?

[zh]
珠峰的北坡在西藏，南坡在尼泊尔。山顶本身是两国共享的。最早被公认登上珠峰的人，是新西兰的希拉里和尼泊尔向导丹增，他们是从南坡上去的。而从北坡登顶的第一批中国登山队员，也在一九六〇年完成了非常了不起的攀登。
[/zh]
[en]
The north side of Everest is in Tibet, China. The south side is in Nepal. The summit itself sits on the border, so it is shared by two countries.

The first people widely accepted as reaching the top were Edmund Hillary from New Zealand and Tenzing Norgay, a Nepali Sherpa guide. They climbed from the south side in 1953.

Chinese climbers also made an important climb from the north side. In 1960, a Chinese team reached the top from Tibet. That was a very difficult and important moment in China's climbing history.
[/en]
[/section]

heading_en: More Than One High Peak

caption_zh: 青藏高原和喜马拉雅周边，不只一座高峰
caption_en: The Himalayas and Qinghai-Tibet Plateau hold more than one great peak.
[zh]
青藏高原上远不止一座珠峰。世界上超过八千米的山峰一共十四座，其中有不少完全或部分位于中国境内——它们大多集中在喜马拉雅山脉和周边。出了青藏高原往东，还有昆仑山、天山、长白山。李白写过"窗含西岭千秋雪"，说的就是四川那种很特别的感觉：在城市里抬头，远远就能望见雪山。
[/zh]
[en]
Everest is not the only great mountain on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. Around the world, there are 14 mountains higher than 8,000 meters. Quite a few are fully or partly inside China's borders. Most are in the Himalayas and nearby mountain areas.

Farther east, China also has the Kunlun Mountains, the Tianshan Mountains, and Changbai Mountain.

The poet Li Bai once wrote about seeing snow on faraway western mountains from a window. That line gives a feeling many people in Sichuan know well: you may be in a city, then look up and see snowy mountains far away.
[/en]

heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: Summary
[zh]
珠穆朗玛峰八千八百多米，站在中国和尼泊尔的交界处。它不是一座孤零零的山，而是整个喜马拉雅和青藏高原壮阔景色里最醒目的那一座。高山、冰雪、森林和深谷，一层一层叠在一起，这就是亚洲最震撼的屋脊风景。
[/zh]
[en]
Qomolangma, or Mount Everest, is more than 8,800 meters high and stands on the border between China and Nepal. It is not a lonely mountain by itself. It is the most famous peak in the huge world of the Himalayas and the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. Snow peaks, deep valleys, forests, wind, and ice stack up layer by layer. This is one of Asia's most breathtaking mountain scenes.
[/en]
[/ending]
