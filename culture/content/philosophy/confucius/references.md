## The Analects, “Wei Ling Gong”

Source: Chinese Text Project
URL: https://ctext.org/analects/wei-ling-gong/zhs
Accessed: 2026-08-20

Supports:

- Zigong asks whether one saying can guide a person for life.
- Confucius answers: “其恕乎！己所不欲，勿施于人。”

Editorial boundary:

- Safe to present as a dialogue recorded in *The Analects*.
- Do not invent a location, time of day, surrounding actions, or private thoughts.

## Confucius and the Formation of *The Analects*

Source: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Confucius”
URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/confucius/
Accessed: 2026-08-20

Supports:

- The traditional figures of 3,000 students and 72 notable disciples come through later biographical traditions and should not be presented as a verifiable class list.
- The formation and transmission of *The Analects* are historically complex; it should not be described as a book personally written by Confucius or simply compiled by his students immediately after his death.
