# Confucius Shared Story and Facts Brief

## Core ideas and evidence

1. Confucius lived more than 2,500 years ago in the state of Lu, near present-day Qufu in Shandong.
2. He studied and taught in a time when many states competed and sometimes fought.
3. He cared about learning, promises, reflection, and how people treat one another. `Ren` and `shu` cannot be reduced to one exact English word.
4. *The Analects* records Zigong asking for one idea to use throughout life. Confucius answers with `shu` and the teaching commonly rendered, “What you do not want done to yourself, do not do to others.”
5. This teaching asks people to consider another person's experience. It does not forbid rules, self-protection, disagreement, or asking adults for help.
6. Confucius did not personally write *The Analects*. Material connected with him circulated and was gathered over time after his death.
7. The traditional numbers of 3,000 students and 72 outstanding disciples are famous, but they are not a verifiable class list.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent a classroom scene, private thoughts, or extra dialogue between Confucius and Zigong.
- Modern examples involving blocks, secrets, lines, and shared toys are teaching examples, not ancient episodes.
- Do not define `ren` or `shu` as if one English word captures the whole concept.
- Do not present Confucius or *The Analects* as the only important voice in Chinese thought.

## Level assignments

- Early Reader: center the question “How would you feel?” Use familiar school and play examples. Briefly identify Confucius and the old book without explaining textual history in detail.
- Story: include Lu, teaching without a modern classroom, Zigong's question, `shu`, practice, and the formation of *The Analects*.
- Advanced: preserve the evidence, uncertainty, historical setting, interpretive limits, and the distinction between the recorded saying and modern examples.
