heading_en: A Very Large Fleet
image: great-fleet-at-sea.webp
caption_zh: 郑和带着很大的船队远航。很多船一起出现在海上，场面一定非常壮观。
caption_en: Zheng He sailed with a very large fleet. Seeing so many ships together on the sea must have been astonishing.
[zh]
明朝的时候，有一位很有名的航海家，名字叫郑和。

他带着一支很大的船队出海远航，船很多，人也很多。

这支船队去过东南亚、印度、阿拉伯半岛，最远还到过非洲东海岸。

在那个年代，这是一件非常了不起的事。
[/zh]
[en]
During the Ming dynasty, there was a famous sailor named Zheng He.

He led a very large fleet across the seas, with many ships and many people.

His voyages reached Southeast Asia, India, the Arabian Peninsula, and even the east coast of Africa.

For that time in history, this was an amazing achievement.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 带去的是礼物
heading_en: He Brought Gifts
image: peaceful-gift-exchange.webp
caption_zh: 船队常常带着丝绸、瓷器和别的礼物去见远方的人，也会收到很多特别的回礼。
caption_en: The fleet often brought silk, porcelain, and other gifts, and it also received many unusual gifts in return.
[zh]
郑和的船队不是主要去打仗的。

他们带去的常常是丝绸、瓷器、茶叶、铜钱和别的礼物。

当地的国王也会回赠香料、宝石、象牙，甚至长颈鹿这样少见的动物。

所以很多人记得郑和，不只是因为他航海很远，也因为他的船队常常带着和平的意思。
[/zh]
[en]
Zheng He's fleet was not mainly sent out to conquer other places.

It often brought gifts such as silk, porcelain, tea, copper coins, and books.

Local rulers gave gifts back too, including spices, jewels, ivory, and even rare animals like giraffes.

That is why many people remember Zheng He not only for traveling far, but also for bringing a message of peace.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3

heading_zh: 从云南孩子到大船队统帅
heading_en: From a Boy in Yunnan to a Fleet Commander

caption_zh: 要带这么大的船队走这么远，光有勇气不够，还要会计划、会看人，也会做决定。
caption_en: Leading such a great fleet took more than courage. It also required planning, judgment, and calm decisions.
[zh]
郑和小时候的生活并不轻松。

可是后来，他一步一步得到信任，最后成了大船队的统帅。

他会和很多地方的人打交道，也能处理很复杂的事情。

一个出身很不容易的人，最后能走到这么远，本身就像一个传奇故事。
[/zh]
[en]
Zheng He's childhood was not easy.

But later, step by step, he won trust and finally became the commander of a great fleet.

He knew how to deal with people from many different places and how to handle difficult work.

The fact that someone with such a hard beginning could travel so far already feels like a legend.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 为什么今天还记得他？
heading_en: Why Is He Still Remembered?
[zh]
郑和一共远航了七次。

后来，这样的大航海停下来了，可是在东南亚很多地方，人们还是会记得他的名字。

有些地方还留下了和他有关的庙、井和传说。

这说明他的航海不只是走过了海面，也留在了很多人的记忆里。
[/zh]
[en]
Zheng He sailed on seven great voyages.

Later, these huge expeditions came to an end, but people in many parts of Southeast Asia still remembered his name.

Some places even kept temples, wells, and stories connected with him.

That shows that his voyages traveled not only across the sea, but also into people's memories.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 一路走到很远很远
heading_en: A Sailor Who Reached Very Far
[zh]
郑和是中国历史上很特别的航海家。

他带着大船队看见了很远的世界，也让很多地方的人看见了明朝中国。

所以今天讲到远航和交流，很多人还是会想到郑和。
[/zh]
[en]
Zheng He is one of the most remarkable sailors in Chinese history.

He took a great fleet to faraway places and also helped many lands see Ming China.

That is why people still think of Zheng He when they talk about long voyages and meeting the wider world.
[/en]
[/ending]
