[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 月亮和诗
heading_en: The Moon and Poems
image: moon-and-poems.webp
caption_zh: 在很多人的想象里，李白总是和月亮、诗句，还有安静发亮的夜晚连在一起。
caption_en: In many people's imaginations, Li Bai is always linked with moonlight, poems, and bright quiet nights.
[zh]
李白是中国最有名的诗人之一。

很多中国孩子很小的时候，就会背他的《静夜思》。

在李白的诗里，月亮常常出现，酒也常常出现。

所以很多人一想到李白，就会想到一个爱看月亮、也爱写诗的人。
[/zh]
[en]
Li Bai is one of the most famous poets in China.

Many Chinese children learn his poem "Quiet Night Thought" when they are still very young.

The moon appears again and again in his poems, and so does wine.

That is why many people picture Li Bai as a poet who loved moonlight and words.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 走过很多山和水
heading_en: He Traveled Through Mountains and Rivers
image: mountains-rivers-waterfall.webp
caption_zh: 李白走过很多地方，也把高山、大河和瀑布写进了诗里。
caption_en: Li Bai traveled to many places and carried mountains, rivers, and waterfalls into his poems.
[zh]
李白年轻的时候，就离开家到很远的地方旅行。

他看过高山、长江、瀑布，也把这些景色写进了诗里。

有时候，他会把看到的风景写得特别大、特别亮，好像整片天地都在动。

这也是为什么他的诗常常让人一读就记住。
[/zh]
[en]
When Li Bai was young, he left home and traveled far away.

He saw mountains, the Yangtze River, and huge waterfalls, and he turned those sights into poems.

Sometimes he wrote about nature in a way that felt huge and shining, as if the whole world were moving.

That is one reason his poems are so easy to remember.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 皇宫留不住他
heading_en: The Palace Could Not Hold Him
image: palace-could-not-hold-him.webp
caption_zh: 皇宫很华丽，可是李白的心常常还是更想飞向外面的山水和远方。
caption_en: The palace was grand, but Li Bai's heart still leaned toward mountains, rivers, and the wide world outside.
[zh]
李白也进过皇宫，为皇帝写过诗。

可是他不太喜欢一直待在规矩很多的地方。

比起站在宫里，他更喜欢继续远游，去看山水，去交朋友，去喝酒写诗。

所以后来的人常常把他记成一个很自由的人。
[/zh]
[en]
Li Bai also spent time in the imperial palace and wrote poems for the emperor.

But he did not enjoy staying in a place filled with rules.

More than palace life, he loved traveling, seeing rivers and mountains, meeting friends, and writing poems.

That is why later generations often remembered him as a very free spirit.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 为什么大家一直记得他？
heading_en: Why Do People Still Remember Him?
[zh]
李白写朋友，也写月亮，还写一个人站在大自然面前突然被打动的感觉。

他能把心里的感觉一下子写得很明亮。

所以过了很久很久，还是有很多人会念他的诗，也会喜欢他的名字。

他像把风、月亮和远方都带进了中国诗歌里。
[/zh]
[en]
Li Bai wrote about friends, the moon, and the sudden feelings that rise when a person stands before nature.

He had a gift for making those feelings bright and alive.

So even after many hundreds of years, people still recite his poems and remember his name.

He seems to have carried wind, moonlight, and faraway places into Chinese poetry.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 一个会发光的诗人
heading_en: A Poet Who Still Shines
[zh]
李白不是只会写字的人。

在很多人的想象里，他还是一个爱远行、爱月亮、爱自由的人。

这就是为什么他一直是中国文学里最亮的名字之一。
[/zh]
[en]
Li Bai was not only a man who wrote poems.

In many people's imaginations, he is also a traveler who loved moonlight, freedom, and the wide world.

That is why his name remains one of the brightest in Chinese literature.
[/en]
[/ending]
