[zh]
很久以前，有一个姑娘叫花木兰。

有一天，她看见征兵的文书，上面有她父亲的名字。

可是父亲已经老了，家里又没有哥哥可以代替他去。

木兰越想越担心，最后下了一个很勇敢的决定。
[/zh]
[en]
Long ago, there was a young woman named Hua Mulan.

One day, she saw an army notice with her father's name on it.

But her father was old, and there was no older brother in the family to go in his place.

The more Mulan thought about it, the more worried she became. At last, she made a brave decision.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 木兰代父从军
heading_en: Mulan Goes in Her Father's Place
image: farewell.webp
caption_zh: 木兰准备好马和行李，离开家，朝很远的军营出发。
caption_en: Mulan gets her horse and gear ready, leaves home, and rides toward the distant army camp.
[zh]
木兰决定代替父亲去参军。

她准备好马、马鞍和路上要用的东西，第二天一早就离开了家。

一路上，她很想念父母，可她没有回头。

她知道自己是在做一件又难又重要的事。
[/zh]
[en]
Mulan decided to join the army in her father's place.

She got her horse, saddle, and travel things ready, and left home early the next morning.

On the road, she missed her parents very much, but she did not turn back.

She knew she was doing something difficult and important.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 军营里的很多年
heading_en: Many Years in the Army
image: army-camp.webp
caption_zh: 在军营里，木兰和别的士兵一样训练、骑马，也学着坚持下去。
caption_en: In the army camp, Mulan trains, rides, and learns to stay strong like every other soldier.
[zh]
木兰跟着军队走过很远的路，也在军营里训练了很多年。

她学骑马、学射箭，也学着在困难的时候坚持下去。

战争的日子很辛苦，可木兰一直没有放弃。

慢慢地，她得到了同伴们的尊重。
[/zh]
[en]
Mulan traveled far with the army and spent many years training in camp.

She learned to ride, to shoot arrows, and to stay strong when life became hard.

The years of war were difficult, but Mulan never gave up.

Little by little, she earned the respect of the soldiers around her.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 木兰回家了
heading_en: Mulan Comes Home
image: homecoming.webp
caption_zh: 战争结束以后，木兰换回从前的衣服，家人和同伴都又惊讶又高兴。
caption_en: After the war, Mulan changes back into her old clothes, and her family and companions are filled with surprise and joy.
[zh]
打完仗以后，木兰没有留下来做官。

她最想做的事，就是快一点回家，见到自己的家人。

回到家以后，她换回从前的衣服，重新梳好头发。

和她一起打仗的同伴这才发现，原来这个勇敢的战友是一位姑娘。
[/zh]
[en]
When the war was over, Mulan did not stay to become an official.

What she wanted most was to go home and see her family again.

After she returned, she changed back into her old clothes and combed her hair again.

Only then did her old army companions realize that their brave fellow soldier was a young woman.
[/en]
[/section]

heading_zh: 一个勇敢的名字
heading_en: A Name Remembered for Courage
[zh]
花木兰的故事已经流传了很久很久。

人们记得她，不只是因为她很勇敢，也因为她很爱自己的家人。

这就是为什么木兰一直是中国故事里很特别的人物。
[/zh]
[en]
Mulan's story has been told for a very long time.

People remember her not only because she was brave, but also because she loved her family deeply.

That is why Mulan remains such a special figure in Chinese storytelling.
[/en]
[/ending]
