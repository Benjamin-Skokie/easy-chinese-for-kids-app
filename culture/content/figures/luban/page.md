heading_zh: 一片草叶带来的灵感
heading_en: An Idea from a Leaf
image: leaf-inspired-saw.webp
caption_zh: 老故事里，鲁班看见草叶边上的小锯齿，就想到：也许木头也能这样锯开。
caption_en: In the old story, Lu Ban noticed the tiny teeth on a leaf and thought: maybe wood could be cut this way too.
[zh]
传说里，鲁班年轻的时候上山砍柴。

他不小心被草叶划到了手，这才发现草叶边上有很多细细的小锯齿。

他停下来，认真看了很久。

后来，人们常常用这个故事来讲锯子的灵感是怎么来的。
[/zh]
[en]
In one old legend, Lu Ban went up a mountain to cut firewood when he was young.

He accidentally cut his hand on a leaf and noticed its tiny sharp teeth.

He stopped and looked at it very carefully.

Later, people often used this story to explain how the idea for the saw was born.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一个会想办法的人
heading_en: A Person Who Looked for Answers
image: workshop-problem-solver.webp
caption_zh: 在很多人的想象里，鲁班最厉害的地方不只是会做木工，而是遇到问题就会想办法。
caption_en: In many people's imaginations, Lu Ban was great not only because he worked with wood, but because he always searched for solutions.
[zh]
鲁班不只是一个木匠。

在很多故事里，他还是一个特别会想办法的人。

后人把很多木工工具和巧妙做法都和他的名字连在一起。

这说明在大家心里，鲁班代表的不只是做木工，更是“看见问题就想办法解决”。
[/zh]
[en]
Lu Ban was remembered as more than a carpenter.

In many stories, he was also someone who knew how to solve problems.

Later generations connected many woodworking tools and clever building ideas with his name.

That shows that to many people, Lu Ban came to stand for more than carpentry. He stood for finding answers.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 为什么木匠一直记得他？
heading_en: Why Did Woodworkers Remember Him?
image: remembered-by-woodworkers.webp
caption_zh: 后来的木匠常常把鲁班当作祖师爷，一代一代把他的名字传下去。
caption_en: Later woodworkers often honored Lu Ban as an old master and passed his name down from generation to generation.
[zh]
后来，一代代木匠都把鲁班当作祖师爷。

有些地方到今天还会保留和鲁班有关的习俗。

人们记住他，不一定只是因为某一件工具。

更因为他代表了一种本事：多看一眼，多想一步，然后把办法做出来。
[/zh]
[en]
Later, generation after generation of woodworkers honored Lu Ban as an old master.

In some places, customs connected with him still remain today.

People remember him not only because of one tool or one invention.

They remember him because he stood for a special skill: look one more time, think one step farther, and make a real solution.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 一个被记了很久的工匠
heading_en: A Craft Master Remembered for a Long Time
[zh]
鲁班是不是每一样工具真正的发明人，今天不一定都能说得很清楚。

可是中国人记了他很多很多年，这件事本身已经很说明问题。

在很多人心里，鲁班就是聪明工匠的名字。
[/zh]
[en]
Today, we cannot always prove that Lu Ban truly invented every tool later linked to him.

But the fact that Chinese people remembered him for so many centuries already tells us something important.

To many people, Lu Ban became the name of a clever craft master.
[/en]
[/ending]
