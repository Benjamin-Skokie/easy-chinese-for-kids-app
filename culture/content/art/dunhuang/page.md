[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 沙漠里不只有沙子，还有一整座画出来的世界
heading_en: In the Desert, There Is a Whole Painted World
image: 1-mogao-cliffs.webp
caption_zh: 在[[敦煌|Dunhuang]]，人们在山崖上凿出了很多洞窟，还在里面画了很久很久。
caption_en: In [[Dunhuang]], people carved many caves into a cliff and painted inside them for a very long time.
[zh]
中国西北有个地方叫[[敦煌|Dunhuang]]。那里很干，很安静，周围有很多沙子。

可就在这样的地方，人们在山崖上凿出了[[莫高窟|Mogao Caves]]，又在洞里画画、塑像，一画就是很多很多年。

所以敦煌最神奇的地方是，你远远看见的是一片沙漠，走近了才发现，里面藏着一整座画出来的世界。
[/zh]
[en]
In northwestern China, there is a place called [[Dunhuang]]. It is dry, quiet, and full of sand.

But in that place, people carved the [[Mogao Caves]] into a cliff. Then they painted walls and made statues inside for many, many years.

That is what makes Dunhuang so surprising. From far away, you see desert. Up close, you find a whole painted world hidden inside.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一间封起来很久的小洞，后来装着一大堆旧书和旧画
heading_en: One Small Sealed Room Turned Out to Hold Many Old Treasures
image: 2-library-cave-scrolls.webp
caption_zh: 有一年，人们打开了一间封住很久的小洞，里面放着很多古代的纸卷、布和画。
caption_en: One year, people opened a small room that had been sealed for a long time. Inside were old scrolls, cloth, and paintings.
[zh]
一九〇〇年，有人无意中打开了一间封住很久的小洞。里面放着很多古代的纸卷、文书、布和画。

这些东西在黑暗里安安静静睡了差不多一千年，所以大家后来都觉得，这是一次很大的发现。

可惜后来有不少东西被带去了别的国家。今天，很多人一说到敦煌，心里既会觉得惊喜，也会觉得有一点心疼。
[/zh]
[en]
In 1900, someone accidentally opened a small room that had been sealed for a long time. Inside were old scrolls, papers, cloth, and paintings.

Those things had rested quietly in the dark for almost a thousand years, so people later felt it was a very great discovery.

Sadly, many of the objects were later taken to other countries. So when many people think about Dunhuang, they feel both wonder and sadness.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 敦煌最有名的身影，是没有翅膀也能飞的飞天
heading_en: Dunhuang's Most Famous Figures Are the Flying Feitian
image: 3-feitian-mural.webp
caption_zh: [[飞天|Feitian]]穿着长长的飘带，没有翅膀，却好像真的在空中转动。
caption_en: [[Feitian]] wear long ribbons. They have no wings, yet they still seem to turn and float in the air.
[zh]
敦煌壁画里最有名的形象，叫[[飞天|Feitian]]。她们没有翅膀，可衣带一飘起来，就像真的飞在空中。

有的飞天在弹琴，有的在撒花，还有的像在风里转圈。孩子一看，常常一下就记住了。

这也是敦煌最厉害的地方。它不是只会讲很老很远的故事，它还会让一面墙忽然变得很轻、很活。
[/zh]
[en]
The most famous figures in Dunhuang murals are called [[Feitian]]. They have no wings, but when their ribbons stream out, they seem to be truly flying.

Some are playing music. Some are scattering flowers. Some seem to be turning in the wind. Children often remember them right away.

That is one of Dunhuang's special gifts. It does not only tell old stories. It can also make a wall suddenly feel light and alive.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 这些画已经很老了，所以今天最重要的事，是慢慢把它们保护好
heading_en: These Paintings Are Very Old, So Today People Work Slowly to Protect Them
[zh]
敦煌的画已经在沙漠里待了很久很久。颜色会慢慢变暗，墙面也会一点点变老。

所以今天有很多人专门研究怎么保护它们。大家不能随便去碰，也不能让太多风、光和灰尘跑进去。

也正因为有人一直小心地保护，我们今天才能继续看到这座没有安静下去的千年画廊。
[/zh]
[en]
Dunhuang's paintings have stayed in the desert for a very long time. Their colors slowly grow darker, and the walls slowly grow old.

So today, many people study how to protect them. Visitors cannot touch them, and people must be careful about light, wind, and dust.

Because people keep guarding them so carefully, we can still see this thousand-year gallery today.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
敦煌在沙漠里，可里面装着很多很多画和故事。

从山崖上的洞窟，到轻轻飞起来的[[飞天|Feitian]]，敦煌告诉我们：有些地方外面看起来很安静，里面却藏着一个很大的世界。
[/zh]
[en]
Dunhuang stands in the desert, but inside it holds many paintings and many stories.

From the caves in the cliff to the floating [[Feitian]], Dunhuang shows us that a place can look quiet on the outside and still hide a very big world within.
[/en]
[/ending]
