[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 只有黑色，也能画出山、水和风
heading_en: One Color Can Still Paint Mountains and Water
image: 1-ink-landscape.webp
caption_zh: 水墨画里常常没有很多颜色，可深深浅浅的墨，已经能让你看见山、水、雾和远处的树。
caption_en: Ink wash paintings may use only black ink, yet different shades can still show mountains, water, mist, and faraway trees.
[zh]
很多孩子第一次看水墨画，都会先愣一下：怎么没有蓝天，没有绿树，也没有红花？

可看久一点，你就会发现，画里虽然只有墨和纸，眼睛却还是能认出山、水、树，还有远远近近的空气。

这就是水墨画神奇的地方。它不用很多颜色，却还是能把一个世界慢慢画出来。
[/zh]
[en]
The first time many children see an ink wash painting, they stop for a moment. Where is the blue sky? Where are the green trees? Where are the red flowers?

But if you keep looking, your eyes still begin to find mountains, water, trees, and even the feeling of far and near space.

That is the magic of ink wash painting. It does not need many colors to slowly build a whole world.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一块墨，也能有很多很多深浅
heading_en: One Ink Stick Can Make Many Different Shades
image: 2-ink-shades-brush.webp
caption_zh: 墨不是只有一种黑。浓一点、淡一点、干一点、湿一点，看上去就会很不一样。
caption_en: Ink is not only one black. Darker, lighter, drier, or wetter ink can all look different.
[zh]
中国画家常说，墨不是只有一种黑。墨可以很浓，也可以很淡；可以很湿，也可以很干。

浓浓的墨，常常让山石看起来更近、更重。淡淡的墨，会让远山像雾里一样轻轻退开。干一点的笔，会让树枝显得更老、更硬。湿一点的笔，又会让水气和云气软下来。

所以画家手里虽然只是一支笔、一块墨，可用出来的变化却很多。孩子看见的不是“黑色一样不一样”，而是“这块地方离我近不近，那座山远不远”。
[/zh]
[en]
Chinese painters often say that ink is not only one kind of black. It can be dark or pale, dry or wet.

Dark ink can make rocks feel close and heavy. Pale ink can make a far mountain drift back like mist. A drier brush can make a branch look older and rougher. A wetter brush can soften water and clouds.

So even when a painter is holding only one brush and one ink stick, the changes can be huge. A child is not only seeing "different blacks." The child is also feeling what is near, what is far, what is hard, and what is soft.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 有时候，最妙的地方反而是没画的地方
heading_en: Sometimes the Best Part Is the Blank Space
image: 3-blank-river-boat.webp
caption_zh: 纸上留出一大片空白，有时候不是“没画完”，而是在让你自己把水、雾和天空想出来。
caption_en: A large blank area on the paper does not always mean unfinished work. Sometimes it invites you to imagine the water, mist, or sky yourself.
[zh]
水墨画里有一个很重要的本事，叫留白。意思不是忘了画，而是故意把一块地方空出来。

比如，纸上只画一只小船，下面一大片白纸，你就会觉得那是水。山顶上方没有画满，你又会觉得那里是天空，是云，是雾。

所以水墨画不是什么都替你说完。它常常画到一半，就轻轻停下来，把剩下的一点想象留给你自己。
[/zh]
[en]
Ink wash painting uses an important idea called leaving blank space. It does not mean the painter forgot to finish. It means the painter chose to leave part of the paper open.

For example, a small boat may be painted on one side, with a wide white area below it. Your mind quickly turns that blank paper into water. A mountain may stop before the top of the page, and the empty space above becomes sky, cloud, or mist.

So ink wash painting does not tell you every last thing. Very often, it paints part of the world and then quietly lets your imagination finish the rest.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 它不像大声说话，更像轻轻请你自己去看
heading_en: It Feels Less Like Shouting and More Like Inviting
[zh]
有些画一下就很热闹，颜色很多，什么都摆在你面前。水墨画常常不是这样。它比较安静，也比较慢。

可也正因为安静，你会忍不住多看一会儿。看着看着，山出来了，风也好像出来了，连没有画出来的那片空白，都开始有了味道。
[/zh]
[en]
Some paintings are loud right away. They use many colors and place everything in front of you at once. Ink wash painting often works differently. It is quieter, and it asks for more time.

But that quietness is part of why it stays in the mind. If you keep looking, the mountain appears more clearly, the wind almost seems to arrive, and even the blank space begins to feel full of meaning.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
水墨画最有意思的地方，是它用很少的东西画出很多感觉。只有墨和纸，也能让你看见山、水、远近和天气。

所以它的魔法，不只是“黑色也能画画”，更是它知道什么时候该落笔，什么时候该停下来，把想象留给你。
[/zh]
[en]
What makes ink wash painting so interesting is the way it uses very little to create a great deal. With only ink and paper, it can still show mountains, water, distance, and weather.

So its magic is not only that black ink can paint a picture. Its deeper magic is knowing when to paint and when to stop, leaving part of the world for you to imagine.
[/en]
[/ending]
