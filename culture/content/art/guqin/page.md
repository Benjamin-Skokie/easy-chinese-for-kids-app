[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 它不是为了热闹的大舞台，更像一个人安安静静地说心里话
heading_en: It Was Not Made for a Big Stage, but for Quiet Thoughts
image: 1-guqin-study-room.webp
caption_zh: 古琴常常出现在书房里。它不是很响，却很适合一个人慢慢弹。
caption_en: The guqin often sits in a study. It is not loud, but it is good for one person playing slowly and quietly.
[zh]
古琴是中国很古老的一种琴。它有七根弦，声音轻轻的，不会一下冲出来。

所以古琴常常不是给很多人一起听的。它更像一个人坐下来，慢慢把心里的话弹出来。

古代常说“琴棋书画”，琴排在最前面。对很多读书人来说，古琴不只是乐器，也是一种让自己安静下来的方法。
[/zh]
[en]
The *guqin* is a very old Chinese instrument. It has seven strings, and its sound is soft. It does not rush out all at once.

That is why the guqin was often not for a big crowd. It was more like one person sitting down and slowly playing out what was inside the heart.

People long ago often spoke of four arts: qin, chess, calligraphy, and painting. Qin came first. For many scholars, the guqin was not only an instrument. It was also a way to grow calm.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 这张琴已经很老了，可它的样子一直没怎么变
heading_en: It Is Very Old, but Its Shape Has Changed Very Little
image: 2-guqin-closeup-strings.webp
caption_zh: 古琴有七根弦，还有一排小小的徽位。手轻轻一滑，声音会像还没完全消失。
caption_en: A guqin has seven strings and small position marks. When the hand slides, the sound can seem to stay in the air.
[zh]
古琴已经有几千年的历史了。直到今天，它还是一块长木板、七根弦，还有一排小小的徽位。

它最迷人的地方，不只是形状老，而是声音很会“留住一点点”。手指轻轻按一下、滑一下，声音像快要消失了，却又还没有完全走掉。

很多孩子第一次听古琴，会觉得它不像在“唱一首歌”，更像在慢慢讲一个很安静的故事。
[/zh]
[en]
The guqin has been around for thousands of years. Even today, it is still a long wooden body, seven strings, and a row of small position marks.

Its magic is not only that it is old. It is also that the sound knows how to stay a little longer. A finger presses or slides, and the note seems almost gone, but not fully gone.

When many children hear the guqin for the first time, it does not feel like a song they can sing along to. It feels more like a very quiet story.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 有个故事说，真正听懂你的人，叫“知音”
heading_en: A True Friend Who Understands You Is Called Zhiyin
image: 3-boya-zhongziqi.webp
caption_zh: 传说里，伯牙弹琴，钟子期一听就明白他在想高山和流水。后来，人们把懂自己的人叫“知音”。
caption_en: In the old story, Bo Ya played, and Zhong Ziqi understood mountains and flowing water at once. Later, people called a true understanding friend a *zhiyin*.
[zh]
古琴最有名的故事之一，是[[伯牙|Bo Ya]] 和 [[钟子期|Zhong Ziqi]]。

有一天，[[伯牙|Bo Ya]] 弹琴时，[[钟子期|Zhong Ziqi]] 一听，就说出了琴声里像高山、像流水的感觉。伯牙很吃惊，因为终于有人真正听懂了他的心思。

后来，中国人常常把真正懂自己的人叫“知音”。这个词，就是从古琴故事里来的。
[/zh]
[en]
One of the most famous guqin stories is about [[Bo Ya]] and [[Zhong Ziqi]].

One day, [[Bo Ya]] played the qin, and [[Zhong Ziqi]] heard mountains and flowing water inside the music. Bo Ya was amazed, because someone had truly understood what he meant.

Later, people in Chinese began to call a friend who truly understands your heart a *zhiyin*. That word comes from this guqin story.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 所以古琴弹出来的，不只是旋律，也是一种人的性格
heading_en: So the Guqin Plays More Than Melody
[zh]
古琴不靠大声取胜。它靠的是安静、停顿，还有手指在弦上的一点点变化。

也因为这样，很多人觉得古琴最像一次和自己的对话。它弹出来的不只是旋律，也是一种心情和性格。
[/zh]
[en]
The guqin does not win by being loud. It wins with quietness, pauses, and tiny changes under the fingers.

That is why many people feel the guqin is almost like a conversation with the self. It plays more than melody. It also plays mood and character.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
古琴有七根弦，声音很轻，却有很长很深的故事。

从书房里的安静，到[[伯牙|Bo Ya]] 和 [[钟子期|Zhong Ziqi]] 的“知音”故事，古琴一直在告诉人们：有些声音，不用很大，也能让人记很久。
[/zh]
[en]
The guqin has seven strings and a soft voice, but it carries long and deep stories.

From the quiet study room to the story of [[Bo Ya]] and [[Zhong Ziqi]], the guqin keeps showing people that a sound does not need to be loud to stay in the heart for a long time.
[/en]
[/ending]
