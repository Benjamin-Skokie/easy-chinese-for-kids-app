## 1. Script types, brush movement, and individual variation

Source: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, “Chinese Calligraphy,” Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History
URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/chinese-calligraphy
Accessed: 2026-09-04

Supports:

- Chinese writing developed five major script types that remain in use: seal, clerical, regular, running, and cursive.
- A flexible hair brush can vary stroke width through pressure and through the use of its tip or side.
- Speed and pressure change the visual effects of a stroke.
- Writers work within recognizable character and script conventions while producing individual variation.

Editorial boundary:

- Script types are not decorative computer fonts, and they did not simply replace one another in a single straight sequence.
- The article does not claim that a viewer can diagnose a writer's exact personality or mood from one stroke.

Supports:
