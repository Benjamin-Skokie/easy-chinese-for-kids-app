[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 咔嚓咔嚓，一只蝴蝶就飞出来了
heading_en: Snip, Snip, and a Butterfly Appears
image: 1-grandmother-butterfly.webp
caption_zh: 一张红纸、一把剪刀，再加上熟练的手，很快就能剪出一只会让孩子睁大眼睛的蝴蝶。
caption_en: One sheet of red paper, one pair of scissors, and skilled hands can quickly make a butterfly that makes children stare.
[zh]
很多孩子第一次看剪纸，都会觉得像变魔术。一张平平的红纸，对折几下，剪刀咔嚓咔嚓走一圈，再一打开，蝴蝶、花或者小鸟就出来了。

最神奇的是，真正会剪的人，常常不用先画草图。纸在手里一折，心里已经知道下一刀往哪里走。

所以剪纸的好看，不只是因为成品漂亮，也因为它打开的那一瞬间，总像有个小惊喜飞出来。
[/zh]
[en]
The first time many children watch paper cutting, it can feel like magic. A flat red sheet is folded once or twice, the scissors go snip, snip around the edge, and when the paper opens, out comes a butterfly, a flower, or a bird.

The most amazing part is that a truly skilled paper cutter often does not need to draw the whole design first. The paper folds in the hand, and the next cut is already clear in the mind.

That is why paper cutting feels special. The finished piece is beautiful, but the moment it opens can feel like a small surprise taking flight.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 窗户、门边、礼物上，到处都能挂祝福
heading_en: Windows, Doors, and Gifts Can All Carry a Blessing
image: 2-window-flowers-home.webp
caption_zh: 红红的剪纸贴在窗上、门边或礼物上，很多人一看就会想到节日和好兆头。
caption_en: Bright red paper cuts on windows, doorways, or gifts make many people think right away of holidays and good wishes.
[zh]
纸发明以后，剪纸慢慢走进了很多普通人家。到了过年的时候，窗户上会贴窗花，门边会挂装饰，礼物上也会压一张红红的剪纸。

为什么常常用红色？因为红色在很多中国节日里都会让人想到热闹、喜气和好运。

所以剪纸不只是“剪得好不好看”的问题。它也常常是在告诉别人：节日到了，祝福也到了。
[/zh]
[en]
After paper became common, paper cutting slowly entered ordinary homes. At New Year, people might place paper cuts on windows, hang them by doors, or lay one on top of a gift.

Why is red used so often? Because in many Chinese holidays, red makes people think of celebration, happiness, and good luck.

So paper cutting is not only about whether the design looks pretty. Very often, it is also carrying a message: the holiday is here, and the blessing is here too.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 有的剪纸大胆有力，有的细得像丝线
heading_en: Some Cuts Are Bold, and Some Are Fine as Thread
image: 3-bold-and-fine-cutouts.webp
caption_zh: 有的剪纸一眼就很有力气，有的却要走近一点，才看见里面藏着多少细细的小花纹。
caption_en: Some paper cuts look strong at once, while others ask you to step closer and discover their tiny details.
[zh]
不同地方的剪纸，样子也不太一样。有的轮廓很大胆，一只公鸡、一朵花，远远一看就很有劲儿。

有的却特别细，线条细得像丝一样，要走近一点，才看得见里面还有那么多小洞、小弯和小花纹。

这说明剪纸不是只有一种做法。有人喜欢痛快利落的样子，也有人喜欢慢慢把细处剪出来。可不管哪一种，都要靠手稳，也要靠心里先有图样。
[/zh]
[en]
Paper cuts from different places do not always look the same. Some have bold outlines. A rooster or a flower can feel strong from far away.

Others are much finer. The lines may be as thin as thread, and you have to step closer before you notice all the tiny holes, curves, and patterns inside.

That shows paper cutting is not only one style. Some makers love a quick, strong shape. Others love slowly cutting out delicate details. But either way, the hand must stay steady, and the pattern must already be alive in the mind.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 一张纸不大，却能装下很多想象
heading_en: One Small Sheet Can Hold a Great Deal
[zh]
剪纸不需要很多工具，也不需要很大的地方。一张纸、一把剪刀，有时候就够了。

可也正因为这样，它特别考手上的分寸。少剪一点不够，剪多一点就断了。每一刀都像在问：你心里想清楚了吗？
[/zh]
[en]
Paper cutting does not need many tools, and it does not need much space. Sometimes one sheet of paper and one pair of scissors are enough.

But that is also what makes it demanding. Cut too little, and the shape is not finished. Cut too much, and the paper tears apart. Each cut is almost asking the same question: do you already know clearly what you want to make?
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
剪纸最让人喜欢的地方，是一张普普通通的纸，居然能在几刀以后变成蝴蝶、花和祝福。

所以它不只是手工，更像一种很安静的变魔术：纸一打开，一个小世界就出来了。
[/zh]
[en]
What people love most about paper cutting is that an ordinary sheet of paper can turn into butterflies, flowers, and blessings after only a few cuts.

So paper cutting is more than a craft. It feels a little like a quiet kind of magic: the paper opens, and a tiny world appears.
[/en]
[/ending]
