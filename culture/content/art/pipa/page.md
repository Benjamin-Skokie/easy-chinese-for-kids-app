[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 十根手指都忙起来
heading_en: All Ten Fingers Go to Work
image: 3-pipa-fast-fingers.webp
caption_zh: 演奏者用不同的手指动作，让四根弦发出很不一样的声音。
caption_en: Different finger movements give the pipa's four strings many different voices.
[zh]
琵琶（*pípa*）只有四根弦，可演奏者的十根手指全都忙得很。右手快速轮流拨弦，声音像一串雨点；用力扫过四根弦，又像大门忽然被推开。左手推、拉、按、揉琴弦，让音符弯曲、颤动，甚至发出沙沙的声音。

琵琶能轻声讲夜晚的故事，也能模仿马蹄、鼓声和兵器相碰。一把乐器听起来像藏着许多乐器，不是因为里面有机关，而是因为手指有许多不同的办法。
[/zh]
[en]
The pipa (*pípa*) has only four strings, but all ten of the player's fingers stay busy. The right-hand fingers pluck one after another, making a shower of quick notes. A strong sweep across all four strings sounds like a great door swinging open. The left hand pushes, pulls, presses, and shakes the strings, bending notes or making them buzz.

The pipa can tell a quiet nighttime story. It can also suggest galloping horses, drums, and clashing weapons. One instrument seems to hold many instruments inside—not because it hides a machine, but because the fingers have so many ways to play.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]

heading_zh: 画里的琵琶跑到了身后
heading_en: The Pipa Swings Behind the Dancer
image: 1-pipa-dunhuang-player.webp
caption_zh: 敦煌壁画中，舞者把琵琶举到身后，边舞边奏。
caption_en: In a Dunhuang mural, a dancer swings the pipa behind her while she performs.
[zh]
敦煌壁画里，有一位飞舞的乐伎把琵琶举到背后。身体向前弯，飘带飞起来，四根弦却还朝着她的手。这个姿势后来常被叫作“反弹琵琶”（*fǎntán pípa*）。

我们不能从一幅壁画判断她能这样弹多久，但画家显然想让她的舞姿显得大胆、轻盈。琵琶很早就随着旅行者和音乐在亚洲不同地方流动，形状和弹法也不断改变。敦煌这幅画，把乐器的旅程和舞者的一瞬间一起留在了墙上。
[/zh]
[en]
In a Dunhuang mural, a flying musician swings her pipa behind her back. Her body bends forward and her ribbons fly, but the four strings still face her hands. The pose became famous as “playing the pipa behind the back.”

One mural cannot tell us how long a real musician could play that way. But the painter clearly wanted the dancer to look daring and light. Pipa-like instruments traveled with people and music across Asia, changing in shape and playing style. This painting catches both the instrument's long journey and one dancer's flashing moment.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 江上的一夜，诗里的一场音乐会
heading_en: A Night on the River, a Concert in a Poem
image: 2-pipa-boat-night.webp
caption_zh: 白居易的长诗写了一位女子在夜晚的船上弹琵琶。
caption_en: Bai Juyi's long poem tells of a woman playing the pipa on a boat at night.
[zh]
九世纪的一天晚上，诗人白居易（*Bái Jūyì*）在江边听见琵琶声，便请弹琴的女子到船上演奏。她先不肯多说，后来才抱着琵琶坐下。音乐一会儿急，一会儿慢，把她从前的快乐和后来的孤单都带了出来。

白居易无法把声音录下来，只能用文字帮助读者去听。他写大弦像急雨，小弦像轻轻私语，又写许多声音同时落下，像大大小小的珍珠滚进玉盘。这首《琵琶行》（*Pípa Xíng*）让一场早已结束的演奏，在诗里响了一千多年。
[/zh]
[en]
One night in the ninth century, the poet Bai Juyi (*Bái Jūyì*) heard a pipa beside the river. He invited the musician, a woman, to play on his boat. At first she said little. Then she held the pipa and began. The music rushed, slowed, and carried both the joy of her earlier life and the loneliness that followed.

Bai could not record her sound, so he used words to help readers hear it. The large strings were like sudden rain. The small strings whispered. Many notes tumbled together like pearls rolling across a jade plate. His long poem, *Song of the Pipa Player*, has kept that vanished concert ringing for more than a thousand years.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 一把琵琶里的两场战斗
heading_en: Two Armies Inside One Pipa
[zh]
著名琵琶曲《十面埋伏》（*Shímiàn Máifú*）讲一场古代战争。开始时，急促的声音像士兵集合；接着，轮指和扫弦越来越快，马蹄、呐喊和兵器仿佛都冲了出来。乐曲忽然安静下来时，战场也像一下子空了。

真正的战场不可能被四根弦完整复制，听众也不需要知道每一个历史细节。演奏者用速度、力度和音色，把两支大军放进我们的想象里。琵琶不用真的变成鼓、马和刀剑；它只要给耳朵足够的线索。
[/zh]
[en]
The famous pipa piece *Ambush from Ten Sides* tells of an ancient battle. Quick notes suggest soldiers gathering. Then tremolos and sweeping chords grow faster. Hooves, shouts, and weapons seem to burst from four strings. When the music suddenly becomes quiet, the battlefield seems empty too.

Four strings cannot copy a real battle, and listeners do not need every historical detail. With speed, force, and changing tone, the player places two great armies inside our imagination. The pipa does not truly become a drum, a horse, or a sword. It gives our ears just enough clues.
[/en]
[/section]
