[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 几个农民打井时，先看见了一张泥做的脸
heading_en: Farmers Digging a Well First Saw a Face in the Earth
image: 1-farmer-finds-head.webp
caption_zh: 一开始，人们并不知道自己挖到了什么，只知道土里忽然露出了一张人脸。
caption_en: At first, people did not know what they had found. They only saw a human face appear from the earth.
[zh]
一九七四年，几个农民在[[西安|Xi'an]]附近打井。挖着挖着，土里忽然露出了一张泥做的人脸。

大家当然会吓一跳。可继续往下挖，又发现了更多人头、陶马和箭头。

后来人们才知道，他们挖到的不是一两个陶人，而是一支藏在地下很久很久的军队。
[/zh]
[en]
In 1974, some farmers were digging a well near [[Xi'an]]. As they dug, a human face made of clay suddenly appeared in the earth.

Of course they were startled. But as they kept digging, they found more heads, clay horses, and arrowheads.

Later, people learned that this was not one or two clay figures. It was a whole army that had been hidden underground for a very long time.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 最让人吃惊的，不只是数量多，而是每张脸都不太一样
heading_en: The Biggest Surprise Is That the Faces Are Different
image: 2-pit-many-faces.webp
caption_zh: 地下站着很多很多兵马俑。你慢慢看，会发现他们的脸、头发和神情都不太一样。
caption_en: Many warriors stand underground. If you look slowly, you can see that their faces, hair, and expressions are not all the same.
[zh]
兵马俑有很多很多个，排成整整齐齐的队伍。可最让人吃惊的，不只是“这么多”，而是“都不太一样”。

有人脸比较圆，有人脸比较长；有人眉毛很浓，有人神情很安静。站在坑边往下看，会觉得自己像在看很多很多真实的人。

这说明古代工匠并不是只会重复做同一个样子。他们很会在同一种东西里，做出许多小小的不同。
[/zh]
[en]
There are many, many Terracotta Warriors, standing in neat rows. But the biggest surprise is not only "there are so many." It is also "they do not all look the same."

Some faces are rounder. Some are longer. Some have strong eyebrows. Some look calm and quiet. When you look down into the pit, it can feel like looking at many real people.

That shows something important. Ancient makers were not only repeating one shape again and again. They knew how to make many small differences inside one big set.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 今天它们看起来灰灰的，可刚出土时，有些其实还是彩色的
heading_en: Today They Look Gray, but Some Were Once Brightly Painted
image: 3-colored-warrior-restoration.webp
caption_zh: 兵马俑原来不只是灰色。它们身上有过红色、绿色、蓝色这些颜色，只是后来大多褪掉了。
caption_en: The warriors were not always gray. Some once carried red, green, blue, and other colors, but most of that color later faded away.
[zh]
今天我们看到的兵马俑，大多是灰灰黄黄的。可它们刚出土的时候，有些身上其实还能看见颜色。

有红色，有绿色，也有蓝色。只是这些颜色碰到空气以后，很快就慢慢消失了。

所以现在大家看到的，不是它们刚做好的样子，而是颜色退去以后留下来的样子。
[/zh]
[en]
Today, most Terracotta Warriors look gray and yellowish. But when some first came out of the earth, people could still see color on them.

There was red, green, and blue. But after the colors met the air, much of them faded very quickly.

So what people see now is not the brand-new look from long ago. It is the look left behind after the color went away.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 地下还有很多答案没有挖出来，所以人们现在很小心
heading_en: Many Answers Are Still Underground, So People Are Careful
[zh]
兵马俑已经很震撼了，可它们只是[[秦始皇|Qin Shihuang]]陵墓旁边的一部分。

真正的大墓下面到底还有什么，今天大家还不知道。人们只知道，那里可能还有很多重要的东西，所以不能随便打开。

也因为这样，考古学家常常要在“很想知道”和“先保护好”之间，慢慢做决定。
[/zh]
[en]
The Terracotta Warriors are already amazing, but they are only one part beside the tomb of [[Qin Shihuang]].

What is really under the great tomb is still unknown today. People think there may be many important things there, so they cannot simply open it.

That is why archaeologists often have to balance two ideas: wanting to know more, and protecting what is still there first.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
兵马俑是一支站在地下两千多年的陶土军队。

它们的脸不太一样，原来还带着颜色。直到今天，它们还在告诉人们：地下的历史，有时候比地上的故事更大。
[/zh]
[en]
The Terracotta Warriors are a clay army that has stood underground for more than two thousand years.

Their faces are different, and they once carried color too. Even now, they keep showing people that the history under the ground can be bigger than anyone first imagined.
[/en]
[/ending]
