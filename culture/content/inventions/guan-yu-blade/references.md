# Research Notes: Did Guan Yu Really Use the Green Dragon Crescent Blade?

This file does not render on the website. It records the sources, supported claims, and editorial boundaries for the published story.

## Chen Shou, *Records of the Three Kingdoms*, “Biography of Guan Yu”

URL: https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=gb&node=603534
Accessed: 2026-09-05

Supports:
- The early historical account says Guan Yu charged on horseback into the enemy formation and defeated Yan Liang.
- The passage does not name the Green Dragon Crescent Blade or give the weapon an eighty-two-*jin* weight.

Editorial boundary:
- The story says only that the early account does not identify the famous blade.
- The page-2 image is labeled as a modern illustration that imagines a spear; it is not presented as evidence of Guan Yu's actual weapon.

## Zeng Gongliang, Ding Du, et al., *Wujing Zongyao*

URL: https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A6%E7%B6%93%E7%B8%BD%E8%A6%81
Accessed: 2026-09-05

Supports:
- This Northern Song military work includes a section on weapon illustrations.
- The work belongs many centuries after Guan Yu's lifetime.

Editorial boundary:
- The article uses the work only to establish that a recognizable long-handled curved weapon called *yǎnyuèdāo* appears in the Song military tradition.
- It does not claim that the surviving edition proves the exact weapon existed unchanged in Guan Yu's time.

## Luo Guanzhong, *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*, Chapter 1

URL: https://www.china.org.cn/chinese/2020-07/20/content_76290725.htm
Accessed: 2026-09-05

Supports:
- The novel gives Guan Yu the Green Dragon Crescent Blade.
- The novel describes the blade as weighing eighty-two *jin*.

Editorial boundary:
- The weapon name and weight are presented as details from the novel, not verified facts about the historical Guan Yu.
