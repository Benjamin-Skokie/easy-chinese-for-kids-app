# Editorial References: Song Ci

This file is for editorial research and does not render on the website.

## 1. What *ci* is and how it relates to music

Source: Stanford Global Medieval Sourcebook, “To the Tune ‘Still Wind and Waves’” introduction

URL: https://sourcebook.stanford.edu/text/liu_yong_since_spring_came/

Accessed: 2026-09-01

Supports:

- *Ci* developed before the Song and matured during the Northern Song.
- Its oral and musical qualities distinguished the form, and its long and short lines followed tune patterns.

Editorial boundary:

- The opening gives only the basic idea that *ci* words followed a tune pattern. It intentionally leaves out technical rules, long lists of tune titles, and a comparison with Tang poetry.
- The familiar-melody comparison is a child-facing explanation, not a claim that Song performance worked exactly like modern popular music.
