Source: China.org.cn, “Romance of the Three Kingdoms”
URL: https://www.china.org.cn/english/china_key_words/2024-08/29/content_117393750.html
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- The novel’s traditional attribution to Luo Guanzhong.
- Its setting amid the political and military struggles of Wei, Shu, and Wu from the end of the Eastern Han into the Three Kingdoms period.
- Its place among the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature.

Editorial boundary:
