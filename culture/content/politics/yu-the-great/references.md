# Yǔ the Great — References and Editorial Notes

This file is for editorial research and does not render on the website.

## Opening Waterways and Restoring the Land

Source: *Book of Documents* (《尚书·益稷》), “Yì and Jì,” Chinese Text Project
URL: https://ctext.org/shang-shu/yi-and-ji/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- Describes the great flood and Yǔ opening major waterways toward the seas and dredging smaller channels into rivers.
- Connects his work with restoring food production and bringing the land back into usable order.

Editorial boundary:

- The article presents the received traditional account and does not turn its broad geography into a precise modern engineering plan.

## Gǔn, Yǔ, Shùn, and the Beginning of Xià

Source: Sima Qian, *Records of the Grand Historian* (《史记·夏本纪》), “Basic Annals of Xia,” Chinese Text Project
URL: https://ctext.org/shiji/xia-ben-ji/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- Records Gǔn’s nine unsuccessful years of flood-control work and Yǔ being appointed to continue the task.
- Preserves the traditional account of Shùn handing leadership to Yǔ and connects Yǔ with the beginning of Xià.

Editorial boundary:

- This is a traditional historical account written long after the era assigned to Yǔ, not a contemporary project record.

## Passing His Door Three Times

Source: *Mencius* (《孟子·滕文公上》), “Teng Wengong I,” Chinese Text Project
URL: https://ctext.org/mengzi/teng-wen-gong-i/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- Says that Yǔ spent eight years away and passed his own doorway three times without entering.

Editorial boundary:

- The article keeps the doorway scene as told in the *Mencius* and does not add later family episodes to each of the three passes.

## Rivers and Canals

Source: Sima Qian, *Records of the Grand Historian* (《史记·河渠书》), “Treatise on Rivers and Canals,” Chinese Text Project
URL: https://ctext.org/shiji/he-qu-shu/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- Gives another early historical version of Yǔ’s work, describing the opening of waterways and the effort to bring rivers toward the sea.

Editorial boundary:

- The article uses this passage to support the image of wide-ranging water management, not the exact route or tools shown in an illustration.

## Qǐ’s Succession

Source: Sima Qian, *Records of the Grand Historian* (《史记·夏本纪》), account of Qǐ’s succession, Chinese Text Project
URL: https://ctext.org/shiji/xia-ben-ji/zh
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Supports:

- Records the traditional story in which Yǔ’s son Qǐ succeeds to the throne and becomes the next ruler of Xià.

Editorial boundary:

- The article describes the succession as part of traditional history and does not expand it into an archaeological claim about the historicity of Xià.
