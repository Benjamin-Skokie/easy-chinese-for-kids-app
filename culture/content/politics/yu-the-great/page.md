[section]
page: 1
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 一开始，大家只想把水堵住
heading_en: At First, People Tried to Block the Water
image: 1-block-the-flood.webp
caption_zh: 土坝刚拦住一边，洪水很快又会从别的地方冲出来。
caption_en: An earthen wall may stop the water in one place, but the flood soon breaks out somewhere else.
[zh]
大禹治水的故事，总是从一场大洪水开始。传说把这场洪水讲得非常可怕，像是天底下最大的灾难。

故事里，禹的父亲鲧先去治水。他想到的办法是“堵”。哪里的水冲出来，就在那里修土坝，把它拦回去。

可洪水太大了。今天刚堵住，明天又从别的地方冲开。后来，很多人就用鲧和禹父子来对比两种办法：一味硬堵，和想办法疏导。
[/zh]
[en]
The story of Yu the Great always begins with a huge flood. In legend, it sounds like the worst disaster under heaven.

At first, Yu's father, Gun, tried to solve the problem by blocking the water. Wherever floodwater burst out, he built earthen walls to push it back. But the flood was too strong. A wall could hold in one place today and break somewhere else tomorrow.

That is why later people often used father and son to show two different ideas: only blocking, or finding a way to guide the water instead.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 2
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 禹换了办法：给水找一条路
heading_en: Yu Chose a Different Idea: Give Water a Road
image: 2-guide-the-water.webp
caption_zh: 禹不是只想把水压住，而是想把河道疏通，让洪水有地方去。
caption_en: Yu's idea was not only to hold water back, but to open channels so the water had somewhere to go.
[zh]
鲧失败以后，治水的任务交到了禹手里。禹没有走父亲的老路。他走了很远很远，亲自去看山和河。

他慢慢明白了一件事：水总要往低处流。你在这里堵住，它就会在那里冲开。光靠堵，是堵不完的。

所以禹换了一个相反的办法：疏。不是只想把水压住，而是给水找路。传说里，他带人疏通河道、开山导流，让洪水慢慢有地方去。这里面当然有神话和夸张，可“给水找路”这个想法，后来一直被人记住了。
[/zh]
[en]
After Gun failed, the flood-control task passed to Yu. Yu did not follow his father's old path. He traveled far across the land and looked closely at the mountains and rivers. Little by little, he saw the problem. Water always wants to flow downhill. If you stop it here, it may burst out there. If you block that place too, it may break somewhere else again. Blocking alone never really ends.

So Yu chose the opposite idea: guiding. Instead of only pressing the water down, he tried to give it a road. In the legend, he and his workers cleared channels and guided the flood toward places it could flow. The story surely grew larger in the telling, but the idea stayed alive for a very long time.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 3
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 三过家门而不入
heading_en: Three Times Past His Own Door Without Going In
image: 3-three-times-past-home.webp
caption_zh: 路过家门时，他知道家人在等，可还是转身继续往前走。
caption_en: Even when he passed his own home, Yu kept moving because the flood work was not finished.
[zh]
传说里，禹治水一共花了十三年。十三年里，他三次路过自己的家门口，却一次都没有进去。

第一次，他的妻子在生孩子。第二次，他的儿子已经会走路了。第三次，儿子已经长成了少年。每一次，他都只是停一下，又继续往前走。

这不是因为他不爱家人，而是因为洪水太可怕了。每耽误一天，就可能有更多人的房子被淹、庄稼被冲走、家人被分开。自己的家还在等他，可很多人的家，早就已经沉进了水里。
[/zh]
[en]
The legend says Yu worked on flood control for thirteen years. During that time, he passed his own home three times and never went inside. The first time, his wife was giving birth. The second time, his son had learned to walk. The third time, that son had grown into a boy. Each time, Yu paused only for a moment and then kept going.

The story does not tell this to say he did not love his family. It tells it to show how serious the flood was. Every day of delay could mean more homes under water, more crops washed away, and more families broken apart. His own family was still waiting for him. Many others no longer even had a home to wait in.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 4
layout: text-only
heading_zh: 后来，他成了传说里的开国君主
heading_en: Later, He Became a Founding Ruler in Story
[zh]
传说里，十三年后洪水终于平下去了。于是禹成了大英雄，后来又从治水英雄变成了新的统治者。后世常把他和[[夏朝]]连在一起，说那是中国最早的王朝。

这些内容到底有多少是传说，有多少和真实历史交叠，今天还在继续讨论。故事里当然有神话和夸张，可正因为这样，它才不只是一个“谁修了哪条河”的记录，而成了很多人都会记住的古老故事。
[/zh]
[en]
In the legend, the flood was finally brought under control after thirteen years. Yu became a great hero, and later stories remembered him as a new ruler too. Many of those stories connect him with the [[Xia dynasty]], often described as China's earliest dynasty. How much of this is legend, and how much fits real history, is still being discussed today.

The story surely contains myth and enlargement. But that is also why it lasted. It became more than a record of river work. It became a great old story that many generations wanted to remember.
[/en]
[/section]

[section]
page: 5
layout: text-wrap-image
heading_zh: 这个故事留下了两个字：堵和疏
heading_en: The Story Left Two Big Ideas: Block or Guide
image: 4-block-or-guide-comparison.webp
caption_zh: 一边是硬挡，一边是找路。很多人记住的，就是这个差别。
caption_en: One side tries to hold the water back. The other side opens a path. That difference stayed in people's minds.
[zh]
今天，研究地质和河流的人，的确在黄河流域发现过很大的古代洪水证据。这让很多人觉得，大禹故事背后，也许真的有一场可怕的大洪水。

可不管细节到底有多少是真的，这个故事最留下来的，还是“堵”和“疏”这两个字。遇到问题的时候，你是只会拼命压住它，不让它发生？还是去找到根子，给它一条路，让它慢慢化开？

也正因为这样，大禹治水一直不只是一个古代工程故事。它也像一个提醒：有些问题，硬压不一定最有用，找到出路，反而更重要。
[/zh]
[en]
Today, scientists who study land and rivers have found signs of very large ancient floods in the Yellow River region. That makes many people think a real disaster may stand somewhere behind the old story.

But whether every detail is true or not, the part many people remember most is the pair of ideas inside it: blocking and guiding. When a problem appears, do you only press it down and try to stop it by force? Or do you look for the root, open a path, and let the trouble ease? That is why the story of Yu the Great lasted for so long. It is not only about an ancient flood. It is also about a way of thinking: sometimes the wiser answer is not to push harder, but to find a way through.
[/en]
[/section]

[ending]
heading_zh: 小结
heading_en: In Summary
[zh]
大禹治水当然带着很强的传说味道，可它一直能流传下来，不只是因为洪水大，也因为里面藏着一条很多人愿意记住的道理：遇到问题，不一定只会硬压，有时候更重要的是找到出路。

所以直到今天，人们一提到大禹，常常想到的还不只是河水，也是在想一件事：有些麻烦，是不是该换一种办法来处理？
[/zh]
[en]
The story of Yu the Great is full of legend, but it lasted for more than the size of the flood. It lasted because it carries an idea many people want to keep: when trouble comes, pushing harder is not always the best answer. Sometimes the more important thing is to find a way through.

That is why people still remember Yu today. They are not only thinking about river water. They are also thinking about how to face a problem when force alone will not solve it.
[/en]
[/ending]
