# Culture Content Handover

Date: 2026-07-19

## Scope

This handover covers the Chinese-source cleanup pass for `culture/content`.

Goal of the pass:

- Make Chinese source more vivid, story-led, and child-readable.
- Remove over-absolute claims such as `世界第一`, `唯一`, `全世界都...`, `一定`, `永远`.
- Keep factual wording safer where claims are debated or oversimplified.
- Preserve knowledge value while avoiding adult, lecture-like tone.
- Keep Chinese and later English aligned in spirit.

Difficulty mapping used during editing:

- `简单` = K-2
- `中等` = grades 2-4
- `困难` = grades 4+

## Editing Rules To Continue Following

- Prefer native, vivid, child-readable Chinese.
- Do not flatten strong articles unnecessarily.
- If a sentence is memorable and safe, keep it.
- If a sentence is memorable but too absolute, soften it instead of deleting its energy.
- Avoid introducing new hard claims unless well-supported.
- Keep endings warm and clear, not preachy and not slogan-like.
