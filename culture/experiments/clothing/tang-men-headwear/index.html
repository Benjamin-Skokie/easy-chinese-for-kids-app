幞头纱帽：唐朝男子的头饰

Futou: Tang Dynasty Men's Headwear

Page 1

它一开始更像一块裹起来的黑布

At First It Looked More Like Wrapped Black Cloth

唐朝男人头上常见一种东西，叫幞头。

它一开始不像今天的帽子，更像把一块黑布裹在头上。

布从后面绕过来，再在头顶收住，就形成了特别的样子。

所以幞头最早让人记住的，不是“帽子感”，而是“裹出来的样子”。

Tang dynasty men often wore something called a futou on the head.

At first, it did not look much like a modern hat. It looked more like black cloth wrapped around the head.

The cloth came around from the back and gathered at the top, making a special shape.

That is why the earliest thing people notice about a futou is not that it feels like a hat, but that it looks wrapped.

Page 2

软的、硬的、再到长翅的

From Soft to Structured to Long-Winged

可是布太软，就不容易一直保持形状。

所以后来的人会在里面加东西，让它变得更挺一些。

再往后，官帽又继续变化，出现了两边伸出长翅的样子。

一个头上的东西，就这样一步一步从“裹布”变成了更固定的帽子。

But cloth that is too soft does not keep its shape easily.

So later people added support inside and made it stiffer.

After that, official hats kept changing and finally grew the long side wings many people know today.

In this way, one thing worn on the head slowly changed from wrapped cloth into a more fixed hat.

Page 3

骑马的时候，脑后的带子会像旗子一样飞起来

On Horseback, the Bands Could Fly Like Small Flags

要是把幞头放到骑马的人头上，感觉就又不一样了。

马一跑，风一吹，脑后的带子会往后飞。

这样一来，头饰不只是安安静静戴在头上，还会跟着动作一起“演戏”。

所以画里很多将军，看起来总像还没开口，气势就已经先到了。

Put a futou on someone riding a horse, and it feels different right away.

When the horse moves and the wind blows, the bands behind the head stream backward.

Then the headwear is no longer sitting quietly on the head. It starts performing with the movement.

That is why many painted generals look as if their presence arrives before they even say a word.

Page 4

后来，“乌纱帽”还走进了成语里

Later, the Black Gauze Hat Even Entered Everyday Sayings

再往后，黑色官帽变得越来越有名。

人们慢慢用“乌纱帽”来表示官位本身。

所以说一个人“丢了乌纱帽”，意思不是帽子丢了，而是官位没了。

一顶头上的东西，后来竟然变成了语言里的权力符号。

Later, the black official hat became more and more famous.

People slowly began using it to mean the official job itself.

So if someone "lost the black gauze hat," it did not mean the hat was missing. It meant the official position was gone.

Something worn on the head had turned into a symbol of power inside everyday language.

小结

In Summary

幞头一开始像裹出来的黑布，后来越来越定型，还慢慢变成了后来的官帽样子。它不只是好不好看的问题，也和身份、礼仪和权力连在一起。

At first, futou looked like wrapped black cloth. Later, it became more shaped and gradually turned into later official hat forms. It was not only about style. It was also tied to rank, ceremony, and power.
