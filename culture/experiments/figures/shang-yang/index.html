商鞅：让秦国变强的人

Shang Yang: The Man Who Made Qin Stronger

Page 1

城门口的一根木头

A Log at the City Gate

商鞅有一个很有名的故事。

他在城门口放了一根木头，说谁把木头从南门搬到北门，就能得到奖赏。

一开始，大家都不相信。

后来真的有人搬了木头，商鞅也真的给了奖赏。这样一来，大家开始相信他说的话会算数。

There is a famous story about Shang Yang.

He placed a log at the city gate and said that anyone who carried it from the south gate to the north gate would receive a reward.

At first, nobody believed him.

But when someone finally moved the log, Shang Yang truly gave the reward. After that, people began to believe that his words would be kept.

Page 2

他想让秦国变强

He Wanted Qin to Become Stronger

在商鞅来到秦国以前，秦国还不是最强的国家。

商鞅帮秦国改了很多规矩，让种地、打仗和做官的路都变了。

他希望国家更有力量，也希望朝廷能更好地控制事情。

后来，秦国真的慢慢变得更强了。

Before Shang Yang came to Qin, Qin was not yet the strongest state.

He changed many rules there, affecting farming, war, and the way people could rise in rank.

He wanted the state to become stronger and easier for the government to control.

Later, Qin really did grow more powerful.

Page 3

这些新法也很硬

His New Laws Were Also Harsh

可是，商鞅的新法也很严厉。

很多人因为这些规矩感到害怕，尤其是原来的贵族和那些不喜欢被管得更紧的人。

商鞅让国家更强，也让国家更冷、更硬。

所以人们讲到他的时候，常常会同时想到“有用”和“可怕”。

But Shang Yang's new laws were also very harsh.

Many people felt afraid of them, especially nobles and others who disliked being controlled more tightly.

Shang Yang made the state stronger, but he also made it colder and harder.

That is why people often think of him as both effective and frightening.

Page 4

最后，他也没逃过自己的法

In the End, He Could Not Escape His Own System

后来，支持商鞅的人去世了，商鞅自己也遇到了大危险。

他逃跑的时候，连住旅店都遇到麻烦，因为旅店也要按他定下的规矩做事。

最后，他被抓住并死去。

可是他留下来的很多制度，并没有跟着一起消失。

Later, the ruler who supported Shang Yang died, and Shang Yang himself fell into danger.

When he tried to flee, even staying at an inn became difficult, because the inn also had to follow the strict rules he had helped create.

In the end, he was captured and killed.

But many of the systems he left behind did not disappear with him.

一个改变国家的人

A Man Who Changed a State

商鞅不是一个容易让人喜欢的人物。

可是很多人承认，他真的改变了秦国。

这就是为什么过了很久以后，人们还是会提起他的名字。

Shang Yang is not an easy figure to like.

But many people agree that he truly changed Qin.

That is why, even after so much time, people still speak of his name.
