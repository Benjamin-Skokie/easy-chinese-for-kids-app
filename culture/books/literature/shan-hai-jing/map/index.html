这张地图怎样画成？ How we drew this world

《南山经》《西山经》《北山经》《东山经》《中山经》都是《山海经》里的篇章。我们沿着这些方向，把书中的山名、奇兽的住处，以及写明的山水关系放进一张图。书里没有写清的地方，我们就想象那里的景色，以及它们怎样连在一起。图上的距离也是想象的。

The Southern, Western, Northern, Eastern, and Central Mountains are all parts of the Classic of Mountains and Seas. We followed those directions and the book's named homes and waterways. Where the book leaves gaps, we imagined the scenery and how the places fit together. The distances on this map are imagined too.
