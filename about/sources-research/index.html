How the books are used

The textbooks guide lesson order, vocabulary scope, sentence patterns, and progression. Nihao World does not display textbook scans or copy textbook illustrations; its explanations, interactions, visual assets, and organization are created for this project. Nihao World is an independent learning project and is not presented as an official publisher product.

字

Character writing technology & data

Hanzi Writer & Hanzi Writer Data

Created by Chanind · Open-source Chinese character stroke-order animation, practice, and character data

Nihao World’s stroke-order and writing activities use selected, locally stored stroke outlines and median paths from Hanzi Writer Data. The site renders its own learning interface rather than embedding the full Hanzi Writer library. Hanzi Writer is MIT-licensed; its character data is derived from Make Me a Hanzi and distributed separately under the Arphic Public License.
